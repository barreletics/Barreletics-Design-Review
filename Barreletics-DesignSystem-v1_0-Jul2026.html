every pixel.

This document defines the design language, component standards, and engineering specifications for the entire Barreletics website. Every section, page, and feature should be built against this system.

Version 1.0 July 2026 Roboto · Warm Charcoal · Terracotta

01

Color System

A warm, restrained palette grounded in charcoal and natural tones. No pure black on orange. No pure orange on black. Every color must feel premium and intentional.

Rule: Never use pure orange on black or black on orange. All color combinations must pass WCAG AA contrast (4.5:1 for body text). Terracotta (#c45c3f) is the accent — use sparingly for hover states, badges, and emphasis only.

Image Standards

Every image must be normalized so the product occupies the same perceived visual footprint. No raw Shopify CDN images at inconsistent sizes.

ContextAspect RatioMin WidthBackgroundNotes
PDP hero (main)1:1800px#f5f2ecSquare, 4 thumbnails below at 72px
Collection card1:1600px#f5f2ecobject-fit: cover · all products same visual weight
Editorial 50/50Free800px—Full bleed in column
Hero background16:91440px—Lifestyle / studio imagery only
Lifestyle3:4 or 1:1800px—Real instructors, real studio environments
No stock photography. All imagery should feature real Barreletics instructors in real studio environments. The brand aesthetic is warm, natural light — not white studio flash. Avoid dark/moody photography — the palette is warm charcoal, not black.

Designed for activities like paddleboarding, beach yoga, swimming at the beach, and walking around resorts or on vacation, these grippy water shoes deliver abarefoot feel with added protection where you need it most.Barreletics are ultra-light, quick-drying, and breathable—easy to wear in and out of the water without bulky footwear.

Flexible design helps protect the main part of the foot while still allowing natural movement.Grippy on most surfaces; however, as with any footwear, certain wet or slick surfaces may require extra caution.

How Do I Put On My Grippy Shoes?
Pull from the top of the foot — not the straps — to help extend their lifespan.

Cleaning
Warm soapy water, rinse well, air dry. No machine washing.

I saw a person in my Pilates class wearing these and I was curious. After looking them up, I'll admit I was skeptical paying so much for "socks". After a little justification on my head (I've likely spent that much or more on grip socks as I explored which type I liked better and the grips just wearing out), I decided to give these a go. There's a little learning curve wearing them for the first time, however you get used to them. The biggest thing was being used to sliding my foot in socks vs these grip so well you need to pick up your for and move it on the footbar or have your strap in the right place for feet in straps. These are so secure there's no sliding or tie wedgies doing lunges. My side planks, I'm not moving! And these clean up just with a rinse! These are definitely worth the purchase. I can't wait for the black to come back in stock in my size.

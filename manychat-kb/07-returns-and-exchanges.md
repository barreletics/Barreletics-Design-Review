# 7. Returns & Exchanges

**AI Use:** Answer "Can I return?" / "What if they don't fit?" / "Return policy?"

---

## 30-Day Returns

Try them indoors for 30 days. If they don't perform, return for a full refund.

**Condition:** Must be clean, like new, no outdoor wear, no sole damage.

**How to start:** https://barreletics.com/pages/returns-portal

## Exchanges (Better Option)

Not the right size? Exchange to a different size for free.

We ship your exchange at no cost once we receive and inspect your return. Often faster than a refund.

**Start exchange:** https://barreletics.com/pages/returns-portal

## 90-Day Warranty

All purchases are covered by a 90-day warranty against manufacturing defects. If something isn't right within 90 days, reach out — we'll take care of it. Return of defective item not required.

## Need Help First?

Not sure about sizing before ordering? Ask and we'll give you a personal recommendation. Might save you a return.

[Full policy details here](https://barreletics.com/pages/returns)

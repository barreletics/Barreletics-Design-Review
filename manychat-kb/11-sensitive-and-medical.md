# 11. Sensitive & Medical Topics

**AI Use:** Answer neuropathy, plantar fasciitis, balance concerns, age-related questions

## "I have neuropathy / foot pain"

Many of our customers with neuropathy tell us they feel confidently back on the equipment with Barreletics. One customer was back to the footwork series in Pilates after almost a year off due to neuropathy.

You can also wear them with orthotics for extra support. 30-day trial so you can test it yourself risk-free.

## "Plantar fasciitis relief?"

Barreletics provide extra support and shock absorption. You can wear them with orthotics for additional cushioning.

Many customers with plantar fasciitis report feeling more secure and confident on the equipment.

## "I'm older / worried about balance"

You're in great company. We have customers in their 60s and 70s who say Barreletics make them feel safe on the equipment again.

One 70-year-old customer says she can accomplish advanced moves on the Cadillac apparatus because the grip never fails. The secure fit gives confidence to push further.

## General Approach

We can't guarantee outcomes for specific medical conditions, but many customers come to us for stability and confidence. The secure grip helps people feel safe and supported.

If you have specific concerns, we're happy to help you find the right size and there's always a 30-day trial.

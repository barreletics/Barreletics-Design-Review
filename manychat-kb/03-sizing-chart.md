## Fit Notes

**Between sizes or wide foot?**
- 7.5–8 with wide foot → size up to L
- 7.5–8 with narrow foot → size down to M

**Snugger fit?**
Dark Grey, Hot Coral, and Blue run slightly tighter. Black and Light Grey are most forgiving for wider feet.

**Men's sizing:**
Men up to size 10.5 → choose L. For a more forgiving fit, pick Black or Light Grey.

**How to place them:**
Ball of your foot should sit where the foot meets toes. Gently tug the top edge at the ankle for correct placement.

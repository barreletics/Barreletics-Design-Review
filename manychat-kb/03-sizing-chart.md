## Size Chart

*Confirmed by Andrew 2026-08-12. Matches the live site and `docs/09-PRODUCT-KNOWLEDGE.md`.*

| Size | Women's (US) | Men's (US) | Kids' (US) |
|------|-------------|-----------|-----------|
| **M** | 5.5–7.5 | — | 2–5 |
| **L** | 7.5–11 | Up to 10.5 | — |

> **Large starts at 7.5, not 8.** An earlier version of this file said 8–11. 7.5 deliberately sits in both rows — width is the tiebreaker.

## Fit Notes

**The 7.5 overlap — width decides, don't just round up**
- Wide foot at 7 or 7.5 → size up to **L**
- Narrow foot at 7.5 or 8 → size down to **M**
- **8.5 and above → L always**, regardless of width. You need the length.
- Below 7 → **M**

**Snugger fit?**
Dark Grey, Hot Coral, and Blue run slightly tighter. Black and Light Grey are most forgiving for wider feet.

**Men's sizing:**
Men up to size 10.5 → choose L. For a more forgiving fit, pick Black or Light Grey. Medium does not accommodate men's sizing.

**Kids:**
Kids' US 2–5 wear Medium — the same fit as women's Medium.

⚠️ `docs/09-PRODUCT-KNOWLEDGE.md` line 84 says "Youth 4–6 wear Medium," which does not match the Kids' 2–5 in every chart including the live site. Unresolved — ask Andrew before quoting a youth number.

**How to place them:**
Ball of your foot should sit where the foot meets toes. Gently tug the top edge at the ankle for correct placement.

# 8. Shipping

**AI Use:** Answer "How long to ship?" / "Do you ship to X?" / "Shipping costs?"

---

## Shipping Costs

- **Free shipping** on orders over $150 (Continental US)
- **$9.95 flat rate** on orders under $150 (Continental US)
- **Expedited & overnight options** available at checkout
- **We ship worldwide**

## Processing Time

Orders process within 24–48 hours and typically ship the next business day.

**Expedited orders:** Must be placed before 12 PM Eastern to ship same business day. Orders after 12 PM Eastern or on weekends ship the next business day.

(No shipping on weekends or holidays.)

## International Orders

We ship worldwide — 195 countries via FedEx International Connect Plus, typically 7–14 business days. Duties and taxes are charged at checkout, so there's nothing extra to pay when your order arrives.

Two exceptions: international **returns** — the customer covers return shipping. International **warranty replacements** — the customer covers shipping and duties on the replacement.

## Tracking

Once shipped, you'll receive a tracking number. Check it to see where your package is.

## Lost Packages

Once tracking shows delivered to your address, the package is considered successfully fulfilled. If in an apartment or multi-unit complex, check with your building or mail area first.

# 10. FAQ: General

**AI Use:** Answer durability, breathability, use cases, comparing to socks, wearing barefoot vs with socks

---

## "How long do they last?"

Many customers use the same pair for years — some wear them 6+ days/week for over 3 years with zero issues.

Built in the USA. Never loses shape. Never loses grip. Same performance on class 1 as on class 1,000.

## "Will they make my feet hot or sweaty?"

No. Actually the opposite. The open-toe design and lightweight material allow air to circulate freely. Unlike grip socks that wrap your entire foot, Barreletics stay surprisingly cool and breathable.

For hot yoga specifically, a thin full sock works like a towel — absorbs extra sweat without affecting grip or performance.

## "Can I wear them barefoot or with a sock?"

Either way works perfectly. Many love the barefoot feel — direct connection to the surface. Others prefer a thin toeless or individual-toe sock for extra warmth or moisture control.

Just skip thick or full-toe socks — they reduce natural toe splay, which makes Barreletics effective.

## "What workouts are they designed for?"

Built for studio training: Barre, Pilates, Lagree, Reformer, Megaformer, Yoga.

Also great for: Weight lifting, outdoor volleyball, martial arts, barefoot running, boating, paddleboarding, outdoor yoga, beach, resortwear, anywhere you'd go barefoot.

> **Never say:** pool, poolside, pool deck, water park, tidal pool, aqua barre, water aerobics. Pool positioning drives the wrong buyer and creates slip risk on wet tile. Use resortwear · paddleboarding · beach · outdoor yoga instead. Don't invent scenery (rocky coves, tidepools, shell-covered beaches) — beach covers it.

## "Do they really not slip?"

360° all-over grip — not dots, not patches. Grips the entire sole, heel, and edges. Locked-in through holds, transitions, and balance work.

No sliding. No resets.

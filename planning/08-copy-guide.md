## Purpose

Rules and references for anyone writing Barreletics copy — website, email, ads, Help Scout, Tidio, wholesale, social, journal, packaging. All copy draws from the Master Knowledge Base (`07-product-knowledge-base.md`) as the single source of truth for product facts.

## Category Creation in Copy

Every piece of copy should move the reader from "which grip sock?" toward "why grip socks at all?" This applies across all channels — not just the website.

**The shift:**
- Old question: "Which grip sock should I buy?"
- New question: "Why am I still wearing grip socks?"

## Hard Rules — Get These Right Every Time

### Return Policy (30-day)
- **Correct:** "30-day returns on items in new, sellable condition"
- **Wrong:** ~~"30-day studio trial"~~, ~~"Try them in class for 30 days"~~
- Framing: Like buying any footwear — try on for fit, return if they don't work.

### Warranty (90-day)
- **Correct:** "90-day warranty against manufacturing defects"
- **Wrong:** ~~"90-day warranty covers grip-surface wear"~~, ~~"warranty covers normal use"~~

### Free Shipping Threshold
- **Current:** $150
- **Wrong:** ~~$75~~ (obsolete)

### Product Naming
| Use | Don't Use |
|-----|-----------|
| Studio Performance Skin (Closed Sole / Open Sole) | Grip sock |
| Performance Skin, Barreletics | Shoes, sneakers |
| Grippy shoes / performance footwear | Just "sock" |

### Pricing
- Closed Sole: $74 · Open Sole: $74
- Installments: 4 payments of $18.50

### Longevity Claims
- **Correct:** "From months to years, from dozens of classes to over 1,000"
- **Wrong:** ~~Specific class counts as guarantees~~, ~~"lasts X months guaranteed"~~

### Heat/Performance Claims
- **Never claim:** ~~"Heat makes grip better"~~, ~~"Performs better when warm"~~
- These are not approved claims.

## Brand Voice

**Positioning:** Big like Nike. Refined like Alo. Smart like Tesla.

| Do | Don't |
|----|-------|
| Confident, not aggressive | Hype / "crush your goals" / fitness-bro |
| Premium, not pretentious | Medical claims, absolute heat claims |
| Data-backed, not salesy | "Best in the world" / "#1" / superlatives |
| Direct, not wordy | Wall of text, explanations where a statement lands |
| Studio-aware, not niche | Generic wellness clichés |

## Copy Patterns by Channel

### Website (PDP, Collection, Home)
- High-impact statements, editorial brevity
- Slogan-anchored sections
- Customer quotes with name and city
- Data points over adjectives

### Help Scout (Email / Support)
- Warm but efficient — respect the customer's time
- Link to Knowledge Base topics, don't rewrite them
- Category creation embedded naturally ("Performance Skins are designed to...")
- Returns/warranty: precise policy language, no hedging

### Tidio AI (Chatbot)
- Q&A format matching Knowledge Base topics
- Concise answers with option to expand
- Always offer to connect with human support for complex issues

### Wholesale / Studio Education
- More formal but still direct
- Lead with category creation positioning
- Data-driven (sock math, longevity stats, instructor trust)
- Specific discipline terminology

### Paid Ads / Social
- Short, punchy — clear the 3-second scroll test
- Problem/solution or value framing
- Customer quotes as social proof
- Never ~~"studio trial"~~ language

### Journal / SEO Content
- Educational, authoritative
- Discipline-specific (use verified move names from Knowledge Base Appendix A)
- Internal linking to products and collections
- Structure for featured snippets and AI citations

## Anti-Patterns — Never Do These

1. ~~"Studio trial"~~ → "30-day returns"
2. ~~"Warranty covers wear"~~ → "Warranty covers defects"
3. ~~"Free shipping over $75"~~ → "$150"
4. ~~Black and orange design~~ → Warm neutrals + rust
5. ~~"Shoes" or "sneakers"~~ → "Performance skin" or "grippy shoes"
6. ~~Aggressive/pushy tone~~ → Confident, premium
7. ~~Wall of text~~ → High-impact statements, editorial brevity
8. ~~Heat performance claims~~ → Not approved
9. ~~Specific longevity guarantees~~ → "Months to years" range
10. ~~Outdoor/water in hero/primary messaging~~ → Studio-first, outdoor in secondary contexts only

## Content Source Rule

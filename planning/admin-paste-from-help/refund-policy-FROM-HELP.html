Returns & exchanges

Returns and exchanges are accepted within 30 days of delivery.

Performance Skins may be tried on indoors for fit only. Returned items must be clean, unworn, and in new, sellable condition.

  • Performance Skins: return in the original mesh bag with tag (if supplied) and packing slip. Poly mailer OK — no shoe box required.
  • Apparel & accessories: unused, unwashed, tags attached, original packaging, packing slip.
  • US return shipping: $7.95 flat rate, deducted from refund. Prepaid label after approval.

Unsure on size? Check the size chart or Contact us before starting a return.

Size exchanges

Exchanges must be initiated within 30 days of delivery. Wrong size? A size exchange is often faster than a refund. Start through the same returns portal and select Exchange.

We ship your exchange at no cost to you once we receive and inspect the return.

90-day warranty

Every pair of Barreletics Performance Skins is backed by a 90-day warranty against manufacturing defects. Return of the defective item is usually not required.

Normal wear and tear, accidental damage, and damage caused by use or improper care are not covered.

Covered

  • Manufacturing defects
  • Structural failure from defect
  • Item arrives defective

Not covered

  • Normal wear and tear
  • Accidental damage
  • Damage from use or improper care
  • Grip-surface wear from use

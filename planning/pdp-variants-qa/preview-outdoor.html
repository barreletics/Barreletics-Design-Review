    Everything you need to know.

    What is the Outdoor Performance Skin actually for?

    Beach workouts & beach volleyball. Paddleboarding & boating. Outdoor yoga. Resortwear & travel. Hot sand & sun-baked pavement. Anywhere you'd go barefoot — but better.

    How is this different from the studio Performance Skins?

    Closed Sole design for outdoor use. Provides grip, stability, and protection from hot sand. The open-toe design lets your toes spread naturally, giving you better balance, better grip, and a more connected feel underfoot.

    Are they better than water shoes?

    Also a great alternative to water shoes — far less bulky, far more versatile, don't trap sand or water inside.

    Is there any surface I should be careful on?

    Yes. Certain wet tile and stone areas are inherently slippery for any footwear, as are wet polished marble and oiled or waxed floors. Always check wet surfaces for slippage.

    How do I clean them?

    Warm soapy water. Rinse thoroughly. Air dry. No machine washing. No special treatment needed. How to put on: pull from the top of the foot — not the straps — to help extend their lifespan.

    What size should I order?

    M (Women 5.5–7.5) and L (Women 7.5–11, Men up to 10.5). Between 7.5 and 8? Wider foot → Large. Narrower foot → Medium.

    How long do they actually last?

    Customers report 18+ months with daily use and zero grip degradation — many are on the same pair after three or four years. The patented material doesn't wear down like silicone dots or rubber patches.

    What's the return and warranty policy?

    30-day returns on items in new, sellable condition. 90-day warranty covers manufacturing defects only. Free shipping on orders over $150. We ship within 1–2 business days.

**Trigger:** Customer asks about sizing, fit, or which size to choose.

### 5. Product Care

**Topic:** "How do I wash/care for my Performance Skins?"
**Trigger:** Customer asks about cleaning, washing, or maintenance.

**Approved Reply:**

> Hi [Name],
>
> Here's how to keep your Performance Skins in top shape:
>
> **Cleaning:**
> - Machine wash cold on gentle cycle
> - Use a mesh laundry bag for best results
> - Air dry (avoid the dryer — heat can affect the fit over time)
> - Mild detergent only (no bleach or fabric softener)
>
> **Storage:**
> - Store flat or loosely rolled
> - Keep away from direct heat or sunlight
> - Don't leave balled up in a gym bag for extended periods
>
> **Longevity tip:** The injection-molded grip won't peel or flake like silicone dots — it's built into the fabric. With proper care, customers report using their Performance Skins for months to years, from dozens of classes to over 1,000.
>
> — The Barreletics Team

### 10. Performance Skins vs Grip Socks

**Topic:** "How is this different from grip socks?"
**Trigger:** Customer asks what makes Barreletics different from regular grip socks.

**Approved Reply:**

> Hi [Name],
>
> Performance Skins are a completely different category from grip socks. Here's why:
>
> **Grip Socks:**
> - Silicone dots glued/printed onto fabric
> - Dots wear off, peel, or flatten over time
> - Partial grip coverage (dots only where placed)
> - Need replacing every few months
>
> **Barreletics Performance Skins:**
> - 360° injection-molded grip built INTO the fabric
> - Grip can't peel, flake, or wear off — it IS the sole
> - Full-contact traction on every surface
> - Lasts from months to years (customers report 3-4 years)
> - Made in USA with antimicrobial treatment
>
> Think of it this way: grip socks add grip ON TOP of a sock. Performance Skins are engineered from the ground up as a grip surface you wear.
>
> Check out our full comparison: [link to /pages/grip-comparison]
>
> — The Barreletics Team

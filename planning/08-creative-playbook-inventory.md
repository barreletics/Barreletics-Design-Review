### 4E. Why Better Than Socks Framework

From manychat-kb/05-why-better-than-socks.md:

**Sock comparison grid:**
- Grip socks: Silicone/latex dots printed on fabric → compress, bunch, slip in holds → lose traction after washing
- Barreletics: Full-bottom coverage built into structured sole → moves with foot, never bunches → locked-in stability, never loses grip

**Benefit pillars:** 360° grip (not dots), antimicrobial & sweat-resistant, no latex/no silicone, natural toe splay, second-skin fit  
**Status:** CURRENT PRODUCTION

## Longer — Returns & Exchanges (Andrew draft)

RETURNS & EXCHANGES

Returns and exchanges are accepted within 30 days of delivery.

Performance Skins may be tried on indoors for fit. Returned items must be clean, unworn, and in new, sellable condition. Items worn for a workout, class, or outdoors are not eligible for return or exchange.

View our full Returns & Exchanges Policy → `/pages/returns#returns`

## Longer — 90-Day Warranty (Andrew draft)

90-DAY WARRANTY

Every pair of Barreletics Performance Skins is backed by a 90-day warranty against manufacturing defects.

Normal wear and tear, accidental damage, and damage caused by use or improper care are not covered.

View our full Warranty Policy → `/pages/returns#warranty`

Shipping

Order processing — Orders are processed within 24–48 hours and typically ship the following business day. We do not ship on weekends or holidays. During peak periods, allow up to 48–72 hours.

Expedited shipping — Must be placed before 12 PM Eastern to ship the same business day. Orders placed after 12 PM or on weekends ship the next business day.

Free shipping — On orders over $150 within the Continental U.S.

Flat rate — $9.95 for orders under $150 within the Continental U.S.

Expedited & overnight — Available at checkout.

We ship worldwide — duties and taxes are charged at checkout where applicable.

Returns (summary)

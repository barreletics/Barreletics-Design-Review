# 01 — Brand North Star

**Status:** 🔵 Ready for Review
**Last Updated:** 2026-07-18

---

## Vision

Barreletics is redefining how people grip.

We are replacing the grip sock category with a new category of performance footwear — Performance Skins — that makes grip socks increasingly irrelevant.

## Mission

Give every studio athlete a single piece of gear that eliminates the compromise between grip, durability, hygiene, and cost — so they can focus entirely on their practice.

## Brand Positioning

**Big like Nike. Refined like Alo. Smart like Tesla.**

| Reference | What We Take |
|-----------|-------------|
| Nike | Bold statements, full-bleed imagery, athlete-first confidence |
| Alo | Clean whitespace, editorial typography, premium calm |
| Tesla | Data-driven proof points, logical comparisons, no fluff |

## Core Values

1. **Performance over promise** — Every claim is backed by product reality, customer evidence, or verifiable data. No hype.
2. **Category creation over competition** — We don't compete with grip socks. We replace them. The question isn't "which grip sock?" but "why grip socks at all?"
3. **Commitment match** — Our customers already invest serious time, money, and energy in their practice. Their gear should match that commitment.
4. **Durability as design** — One pair replaces eight. That's not a tagline — it's the product architecture.
5. **Studio-first, always** — Primary positioning is studio performance (barre, Pilates, Lagree, Reformer, yoga). Outdoor and water use are secondary contexts, never the lead.

## The Category Creation Principle

This is the foundational strategic principle. It must be reflected in every customer-facing asset.

**Instead of asking:** "Which grip sock should I buy?"
**We want customers to ask:** "Why am I still wearing grip socks?"

This influences: brand messaging, homepage, navigation, collection pages, PDPs, product knowledge base, FAQ, compare pages, journal, email, paid ads, organic social, SEO, GEO/AI search, PR, wholesale education, studio education, packaging, retail displays, and future product launches.

Every asset should move the customer from "which grip sock is best?" toward "why would I wear grip socks at all?"

## Brand Truths

- Grip socks were the first generation. Performance Skins are the next generation.
- Traditional grip socks fail in two ways: silicone dots wear off, fabric absorbs sweat and bacteria.
- Barreletics = full-contact 360° grip, skin-safe, rinse and reuse.
- One pair at $74 replaces 6–8 pairs at $14–$18 each per year.
- Made in USA. Injection-molded sole. Proprietary grip compound.
- Latex-free, silicone-free, antimicrobial, sweat-resistant.
- Trusted by 1,000's of instructors and studios.

## Audience

Studio-committed practitioners who attend 3+ classes per week. They already spend on premium studio memberships, Alo/Lululemon apparel, and quality equipment. They are ready to upgrade from disposable grip socks — they just didn't know a better option existed.

**Primary disciplines:** Barre, Reformer Pilates, Lagree, Megaformer, Yoga
**Secondary contexts:** Weight lifting, martial arts, barefoot running, boating, beach, paddleboarding

## North Star Metric

Move the market conversation from "best grip socks" to "grip sock alternatives" to "performance skins." When customers search for Barreletics by name rather than by category, the category creation has succeeded.

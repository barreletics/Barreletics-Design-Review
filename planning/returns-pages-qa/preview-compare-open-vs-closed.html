Open Sole vs Closed Sole — popular questions

What is the difference between Open Sole and Closed Sole?

Coverage and feel only. The Closed Sole has the heel and foot fully covered. The Open Sole leaves the heel exposed with a mid-foot breathing hole for a more grounded, barefoot feel and natural toe splay. Both perform identically — same grip, same stability.

Which sole should I choose for reformer Pilates or barre?

Either. Both soles perform identically across barre, reformer Pilates, Lagree, and Megaformer — same grip, same stability, same studio uses. Choice is preference and feel only.

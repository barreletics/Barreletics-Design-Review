Fit Notes by Color

Dark Grey, Hot Coral & Blue

These colors run slightly snugger than Light Grey. If you're between sizes in Dark Grey, Hot Coral, or Blue — size up.

Light Grey

Light Grey offers a slightly more relaxed fit compared to other colors. True to size for most customers.

Fit Tips

Between Sizes? Size Up.

The material conforms slightly over the first few wears, but won't stretch dramatically. A slightly roomier fit is always more comfortable than too tight.

Narrow Feet

Try wearing a thin sock underneath for a more secure fit. This fills the extra space without affecting grip performance.

Second-Skin Fit

They should feel snug on first wear — secure but not painful. The material has slight give and conforms to your foot shape over the first few sessions.

Color Matters

Dark Grey, Hot Coral, and Blue run slightly snugger than Light Grey due to material variations. Factor this into your size selection.

Size guide — common questions

What size Barreletics should I get?

Barreletics come in M (US 5-8) and L (US 8.5-11). Between sizes? Size up. Dark Grey, Hot Coral, and Blue run slightly snugger than Light Grey.

Do Barreletics run true to size?

Generally yes, with a snug second-skin fit. Light Grey is the most relaxed fit. Dark Grey, Hot Coral, and Blue are slightly snugger — size up if between sizes in those colors.

Questions from the studio

Grippy socks searches, machines, heated rooms, and shipping — one place. Same grip system in every color above.

What's the difference between Closed Sole and Open Sole?
Closed Sole covers the entire foot including the heel — preferred for reformer footwork series, Cadillac push-through and parakeet, Megaformer Super Lunge, and Lagree machine work. Open Sole leaves the heel open for a barefoot feel on mat Pilates, yoga (chair pose, warrior, downward dog), and hot Pilates. Same patented grip, same $74 — pick the build for your practice, then shop colors above.
Tired of Pilates socks and yoga socks that slip?
Grip socks lose traction when dots fail and fabric stretches — usually mid-hold. Performance Skins replace that category with full-underfoot grippy shoes through flat back chair and water ski at the barre, chair pose on the mat, reformer footwork series, and Megaformer Super Lunge. No sliding. No resets. Not another disposable sock.
Best alternative to Pilates socks, yoga socks & barre grip socks?
Yes — that's the job. Traditional barre grip socks and Pilates socks absorb sweat and lose traction in weeks. One Performance Skin pair (Closed Sole or Open Sole, $74) holds through footwork series, water ski, Super Lunge, Catfish, and heated rooms without the sock-replacement cycle. Full-contact 360° grip instead of dots — built for barre, Classical and reformer Pilates, Cadillac, Megaformer, Lagree, yoga, and hot Pilates. Shop now.
Which machines and disciplines are they built for?
Barre, mat Pilates, Classical Pilates, reformer, Cadillac (trapeze table), Chair, Tower, Megaformer, Lagree, yoga, hot yoga, and hot Pilates. Same 360° grip whether you're on the carriage, holding the Cadillac bar (parakeet / push-through), digging into Super Lunges on Megaformer, or flowing on the mat. Closed Sole for full-foot lockdown on machines; Open Sole when you want heel freedom on mat and yoga days.
Reformer, Cadillac, Chair & Tower — will they hold on Classical apparatus?
Yes. On the reformer: footwork series (toes, arches, heels, tendon stretch), elephant, knee stretches, and the long stretch series demand continuous underfoot contact. On the Cadillac / trapeze table: push-through and parakeet need grip that holds the bar — not a sock that slides off mid-exercise. Bridging and footwork on the Chair or reformer stay locked without sock resets. Closed Sole is the usual pick for full-foot apparatus work.
Megaformer, Lagree & barre — named moves that punish grip socks?
Megaformer and Lagree: Super Lunge, Elevator Lunge, Catfish, Bear, and Plank to Pike load the foot laterally — silicone-dot socks slide. At the barre: flat back chair, water ski, relevé, plié, and seat-work pulses need feet that don't walk out of the hold. Same 360° grip system in every color above.
What about hot Pilates, hot yoga & heated studios?
Yes — for hot Pilates, hot yoga, and humid rooms. Heat and sweat kill silicone-dot Pilates socks and yoga socks. Wear a thin sock or thin liner under the Performance Skin to absorb sweat; the Skin stays the traction layer on the floor or carriage. Open Sole for airflow on mat days; Closed Sole when you want full coverage. Hot Pilates & Yoga Kits (Skin + thin liner) are coming; until then, any thin, low-bulk sock works under the Skin.
Yoga flows — chair pose to downward dog?
From chair pose (Utkatasana) and warrior to downward dog and standing balances — grip that holds through every transition. Open Sole when you want a barefoot heel on the mat; Closed Sole when you prefer full coverage. Same $74 system as barre and Pilates — no slipping in chair pose or through the flow.
How do I clean them?
Wipe with water or mild soap. Non-porous grip doesn't absorb sweat the way fabric Pilates socks or yoga socks do — rinse and reuse. No washing machine required. Thin socks worn underneath for hot Pilates: wash those as you would any sock. More in the Care FAQ.

Product FAQ

What’s the difference between Closed Sole and Open Sole?
Closed Sole covers the entire foot including the heel for maximum protection and 360° grip. Open Sole leaves the heel open for a barefoot feel and more airflow. Same patented grip surface, same $74, same durability — choose based on your preferred feel and discipline.
Will these stop me from slipping in class?
Yes. Barreletics uses a full-contact 360° grip surface — not silicone dots that peel. Locked-in traction through holds, transitions, and balance work on barre, reformer, Megaformer, and yoga mats.
How do I clean them?
Wipe clean with water or mild soap. The non-porous surface doesn’t absorb sweat the way fabric grip socks do — rinse and reuse. No washing machine required.
What’s your return and warranty policy?
30-day returns on items in new, sellable condition. 90-day warranty against manufacturing defects (not normal wear). Free shipping on orders over $150. Ships in 1–2 days.
What size should I order?
Two sizes: M and L. Use the size chart for foot measurements. If you’re between sizes or unsure, check the chart linked above the product grid — most studio practitioners find M or L covers standard women’s sizing ranges.

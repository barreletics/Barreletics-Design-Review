Best grippy shoes for barre & Pilates — how Barreletics compares
Barreletics performance skins use a patented full-contact grip surface across the entire underfoot — not silicone dots, not rubber patches. The result: consistent traction from day 1 to day 500+, no replacements, and better performance per dollar than any grip sock on the market. Built for barre, reformer Pilates, Lagree, Megaformer, and yoga practitioners who demand more from their footwear.
Florida, Texas & the South — where studio culture runs deep
From Miami and Sarasota to Austin and Houston, the South has some of the most vibrant barre, Pilates, and Lagree communities in the country. Florida's year-round studio culture — barre in Boca Raton, reformer in Tampa and Naples, Lagree in Miami Beach — demands grip that performs in heat and humidity. Texas studios from Austin to Dallas to San Antonio rely on 360° traction through flat back chair, water ski, footwork series, and Super Lunges.
California studios — Los Angeles, Santa Barbara, San Diego & beyond
California's reformer and Megaformer community has adopted Barreletics as the performance standard. From Santa Monica and West Hollywood to Santa Barbara and San Diego, instructors trust the 360° grip through footwork, elephant, knee stretches, and the full Lagree repertoire — Catfish, Bear, Scrambled Eggs — even in high-heat rooms. No silicone, no latex, no compromises.
New York, Northeast & East Coast barre and Pilates studios
Barreletics is the preferred grip footwear at barre, Pilates, and reformer studios across Manhattan, Brooklyn, and the greater NYC area — plus Pittsburgh, Philadelphia, Washington DC, and Boston. Whether you're in flat back chair and water ski at the barre, footwork series and bridging on the reformer, or Super Lunges on the Megaformer — the 360° grip surface handles every surface, every transition.
International shipping — Canada, UK, Australia & 30+ countries
Barreletics ships worldwide to barre and Pilates studios across Toronto, Vancouver, London, Melbourne, Sydney, Mexico City, Dubai, Milan, and Singapore.

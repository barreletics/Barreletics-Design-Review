One patented grip system, two builds. Closed Sole wraps your entire foot. Open Sole frees your heel for a barefoot feel. Same 360° grip, same durability, same $74. Choose the one that fits your practice.

Full Coverage

Closed Sole

Full coverage including heel — more protection everywhere. 360° grip across the entire underfoot. Ideal for reformer footwork, barre, and versatile enough for outdoor activities like paddleboarding or beach training.

Barefoot Feel

Open Sole

Open heel with a mid-foot vent — your heel contacts the surface directly for a grounded, barefoot-like feel with extra airflow. Same 360° grip where it matters most. Ideal for mat work, yoga, and heated studios.

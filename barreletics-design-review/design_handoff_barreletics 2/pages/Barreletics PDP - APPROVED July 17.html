FAQ

Everything you need to know.

What makes Barreletics different from grip socks?
Your foot moves in the sock. The sock moves on the floor. Now neither does. Barreletics wraps your foot like a second skin with 360° continuous grip from heel to toe — not patches, not dots. The grip surface never degrades, even after hundreds of classes. It's a fundamentally different approach: performance skin, not a sock.
Are these good for reformer Pilates and Megaformer?

Built for every piece of studio equipment — reformer, Megaformer, Cadillac, Pilates chair, barre, mat, and TRX. Barreletics was originally designed for barre and expanded to grip every studio surface. The 360° grip handles the carriage's lateral forces during footwork series, bridging, elephant, and pikes that destroy traditional socks — and provides the same secure hold on footbars, springboards, and apparatus work.

How long do they actually last?

From months to years — customers regularly report hundreds of classes, with some logging over 1,000. The lifespan of any performance footwear varies from person to person depending on frequency of use, workout intensity, studio surfaces, and individual movement patterns. Some customers enjoy their Barreletics for years, while others wear through them more quickly. The grip itself never degrades — the patented material doesn't break down like silicone dots or rubber patches. One pair replaces 6–8 pairs of grip socks per year.

How do I clean them?
Warm soapy water, rinse, air dry. Ready for your next class in under an hour. Never machine wash or bleach. The grip lasts indefinitely with proper care.
What size should I order?
Two sizes: M (Women 5–7.5, Men 6–8) and L (Women 8–10, Men 8.5–11). The stretch-fit material conforms to your foot. If you're between sizes, go with the larger size for the most comfortable fit.
Open Sole vs Closed Sole — which one?
Both builds share the same patented 360° grip, second-skin fit, and natural toe splay — and both protect the balls of your feet. The difference is heel coverage and breathability. Open Sole: open heel with a mid-foot vent — your heel contacts the surface directly, so it feels more grounded, with extra airflow. Closed Sole: full coverage including the heel — more protection everywhere. Same grip, same fit, same durability — choose the feel that fits your practice.
Barreletics vs grip socks — which is better for studio fitness?

Grip socks rely on silicone or rubber dots glued to fabric — after just a few washes, those dots start to peel, flatten, and lose shape. Barreletics performance skins use a patented full-contact grip surface across the entire underfoot that never degrades. The result: consistent traction from day 1 to day 500+, no replacements, far less landfill waste compared to replacing socks multiple times a year, and better performance per dollar than any sock on the market.

"These grip the floor in class while doing lunges and side planks that you're not budging. In the usual half toe socks I'd wear, you'd get a toe wedgie and start sliding." — Amy S., Pittsburgh

"All the other grip socks end short of the heel right where you need it for bridging in Pilates. NOT with these — they've got grip going all the way up your heel. Worth every penny." — Nancy D., Eugene

Barreletics for hot yoga & heated studios

Hot yoga? Hot Pilates? We recommend wearing a thin sock underneath to absorb sweat — your Barreletics grip the surface the same way they always do, while the sock handles moisture. Same traction, same stability, no distraction.

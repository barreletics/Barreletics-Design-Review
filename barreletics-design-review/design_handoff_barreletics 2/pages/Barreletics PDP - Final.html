About this product

What makes Barreletics different from grip socks?

Grip socks have two failure points: the sock slips on the floor, and your foot slips inside the sock. Barreletics is a performance skin — it wraps your foot like a second skin, eliminating interior movement entirely. The exterior grip covers 360 degrees from heel to toe. No latex, no silicone dots, no fabric to stretch out. This is why thousands of barre instructors, reformer Pilates practitioners, and Lagree devotees across the United States, Australia, the UK, and Canada have made the switch from traditional grip socks to Barreletics performance skins.

Best grippy shoes for reformer Pilates, barre, and Megaformer

Barreletics performance skins are specifically engineered for reformer Pilates, barre, Lagree, and Megaformer. The closed sole grips the footbar and carriage through every transition — from footwork series to bridging to pikes. Over 294 verified reviews cite the reformer as the primary use case. Whether you practice at a boutique studio in New York, Los Angeles, London, Melbourne, or Toronto, the 360-degree grip system performs on every surface: hardwood barre floors, reformer carriages, Megaformer platforms, and yoga mats.

How long do Barreletics last compared to grip socks?

Grip socks typically lose their grip after 6–8 washes as the silicone dots crack, peel, and wear away. Barreletics customers report using the same pair for 1–4+ years with no grip degradation. Kimberly from Knoxville has been on her first pair for 4 years — she bought a second for the color. At $74 versus $144–$336 in annual sock spending, the long-term value is significant for practitioners who attend 3–8 classes per week.

How to clean Barreletics performance skins

Warm soapy water and air dry. Never machine wash — machine washing accelerates material breakdown. A 60-second rinse after class keeps them studio-fresh. The non-porous surface doesn't absorb sweat or bacteria the way grip sock fabric does, making Barreletics a more hygienic option for shared studio environments.

Barreletics sizing guide — Medium vs Large

Two sizes: Medium fits Women's 5–7.5 / Men's 6–8. Large fits Women's 8–10 / Men's 8.5–11. The performance skin should sit where the ball of your foot meets your toes. If you're between sizes, go down — the material has slight stretch in the toe box. For men up to size 10.5, choose Large.

Closed Sole vs Open Sole — which is right for your practice?

Closed Sole covers the entire foot — ideal for barre, reformer Pilates, and Megaformer, and required by studios that mandate covered toes. Open Sole leaves the toes exposed for maximum barefoot feel — ideal for yoga, mat Pilates, and floor work where toe articulation matters. Both share the same 360° grip system on the underside. Joseph Pilates designed the practice to be done barefoot; Barreletics honors that philosophy while providing the grip and safety that bare feet and grip socks cannot.

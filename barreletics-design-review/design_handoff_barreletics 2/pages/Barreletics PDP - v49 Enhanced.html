Rinse with warm soapy water after class, then air dry. That's it — no machine washing, no bleach, no special detergents. Barreletics sizing guide — Medium vs Large +

Barreletics comes in two sizes. Medium fits Women's 5–7.5 / Men's 6–8. Large fits Women's 8–10 / Men's 8.5–11. If you're between sizes, we recommend sizing up for a more comfortable fit. The performance skin is designed to wrap snugly without bunching — a close fit is intentional, but it shouldn't feel restrictive. Check the size chart on the product page or reach out to our team if you need help deciding.

Closed Sole vs Open Sole — which is right for your practice? +

Barreletics offers two builds, both with the same patented 360° grip. Closed Sole covers the full foot — ideal for reformer Pilates, Megaformer, and Lagree studios that require covered toes. Open Sole leaves the toes free for a more barefoot feel, inspired by Joseph Pilates' original barefoot philosophy — popular for barre, yoga, and mat Pilates. Both builds grip equally well; choose based on your studio's requirements and how much toe freedom you prefer.

A barre teacher’s shoe routine.

From morning class to evening recovery — what one Megaformer instructor wears, and why.

Priya R.Megaformer instructor & studio owner

Three classes a day, six days a week. Here’s what I actually wear on my feet from 6am call time to the end-of-day cool down.

Morning · 6am Megaformer.

Closed Sole · Onyx. Reformer carriage transitions need full coverage, full grip, and zero distraction. I’ve been on the same pair for 14 months — rinsed after every class, air-dried overnight, still gripping like day one.

Midday · barre.

Same shoes, different class. Closed Sole works for barre too — full upper, secure through plies and balance work. Most of my Pure Barre-style students use these.

Evening · recovery + floor work.

Switch to Open Sole for floor Pilates and recovery flow. The grounded barefoot feel matters for those moments.

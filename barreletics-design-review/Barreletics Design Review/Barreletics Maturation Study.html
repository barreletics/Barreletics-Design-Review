Common questions

Everything you need to know.

What makes Barreletics different from grip socks?

Grip socks have two failure points: the sock slips on the floor, and your foot slips inside. Barreletics wraps your foot like a second skin. 360° grip from heel to toe.

Are Barreletics good for reformer Pilates?

Yes — engineered for reformer Pilates, barre, Lagree and Megaformer. 294 verified reviews.

How long do Barreletics last?

Customers report 1–4+ years with no grip degradation. $74 vs $144–$336 in annual sock spending.

How do you clean them?

Warm soapy water, air dry. Never machine wash.

What size should I order?

M (W 5.5–7.5) and L (W 8–11). For men up to 10.5, choose Large.

What is the return policy?

30-day returns. 90-day warranty — structural failure, we replace it.

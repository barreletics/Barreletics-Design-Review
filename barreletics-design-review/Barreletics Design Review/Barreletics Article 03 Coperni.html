Coperni × Barreletics, in Paris.

Behind the SS26 runway — and what it means to build a Pilates shoe for haute couture.

Editorial teamBarreletics

Spring–Summer 2026. The Coperni runway in Paris. Limited Edition Pilates shoes on the feet of the models, made by us.

Coperni built a language around the body in motion — sculpted shapes, structured movement, technical fabrics. When the collaboration came together, the only question was: can a Pilates shoe live on a Paris runway? Turns out yes.

Designed for the body in motion.

The closed sole, the proprietary grip system, the silhouette tuned for the runway. One run, no second pressing. Available now while quantities last.

What it means.

For us, it’s validation that performance skins belong in conversations about modern design — not just studio utility. For Coperni, it’s a footwear collaboration that respects the body actually moving in it.

Performance skins don’t need babying — but a few habits will roughly double their working life. Here’s the studio routine we recommend.

Most owners ruin their performance skins one of two ways: machine washing them on hot, or putting them away wet after a sweaty class. Both are fixable.

The 60-second rinse

After class, run warm water through the underside of the shoe — not hot, not freezing. Add a small drop of mild soap. Massage briefly, rinse, and squeeze gently. Total time: under a minute.

Drying — the part people skip

Air-dry, never the machine, and never direct sunlight for more than an hour. The grip surface is engineered for studio temperatures; UV slowly degrades it.

06 — You said let’s do it. Here’s how.

Recommended path: hybrid.

Skip a pixel-final mock for every screen — that’s slow and produces a lot of dead-weight artifacts. Instead: I produce desktop + mobile pixel-finals for Home and PDP only (the two pages your dev will agonize over), and the wireframes + audit tokens become the spec for everything else.

Step 1 · this week

Pixel-final Home + PDP

Both pages, desktop + mobile, with real photography slots called out. Includes the full type ramp, button library, and component CSS in usable form for your dev.

Step 2 · next 2 weeks

Dev builds draft theme

Your developer duplicates the live theme, locks the tokens, normalizes the 4 reuse-sections, builds the 5 new sections, recomposes the templates. Phase 1–5 plan in the audit.

Step 3 · review & ship

QA on staging, then cutover

Walk every template on draft theme, compare against live, fix discrepancies, then publish. Snapshot old theme as archive.

Before I start the pixel-finals — confirm these four:

  1. Home leads with media-split hero (video left, copy right). ✅ shown above.
  2. Collection skips the hero and leads with the variant grid. ✅ shown above.
  3. Reviews = curated quote (br-testimonial) + Judge.me grid (judgeme_widgets restyled) on PDP, Home, Collection. ✅ shown above.
  4. One warm accent (#f93820) replaces both sale red and coral. Cart dot, star ratings, sale price, sale badge — all the same warm red. (Open to coral if you want softer; the Tweaks panel on the audit doc lets you test.)

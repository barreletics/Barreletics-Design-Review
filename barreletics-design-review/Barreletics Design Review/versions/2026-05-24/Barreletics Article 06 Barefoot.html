Why barefoot still wins.

Joseph Pilates, traditional practice, and the case for grip footwear that respects the foot.

Editorial teamBarreletics

Joseph Pilates designed his practice to be done barefoot. Yoga has always been a barefoot tradition. So why even wear footwear in the studio? Because some studios require it — and because grip matters more than purism.

The barefoot tradition.

Pilates, yoga, and many movement disciplines were designed for barefoot practice. The foot is meant to articulate, grip, and feel the floor. That’s real.

Where studio rules come in.

Many studios require covered feet for hygiene and equipment protection. Pure Barre. 33 Threads partner studios. Most reformer studios. The rule is convention, not movement gospel.

The compromise that works.

The Closed Sole performance skin honors the rule. The Open Sole honors the barefoot tradition. Both have the same proprietary grip underfoot — you pick the build that fits your studio and your practice.

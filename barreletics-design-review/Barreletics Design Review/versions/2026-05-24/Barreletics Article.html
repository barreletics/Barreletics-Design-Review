How to wash your performance skins.

A 60-second rinse and a counter to air-dry on — the whole care routine, in two steps.

Brian S.Founder & head of product

Performance skins are built to be lived with. A 60-second rinse after class is all the care they actually need — here’s the routine that turns “studio shoe” into “the only studio shoe you own.”

The performance skin is a single-material surface — nothing absorbs sweat, nothing peels off. That makes care almost embarrassingly simple. A quick rinse between classes and you’re reset.

The 60-second rinse.

After class, run warm water through the underside of the shoe. Add a small drop of mild soap, massage briefly, rinse, and squeeze gently. That’s the whole routine.

No special detergent, no special tools, no soak time. The grip surface is engineered to reset itself — sweat runs off, and warm water carries away anything else.

Air-dry, always.

Set them out where there’s airflow — a counter, a windowsill, the floor by an open window. They’ll be ready by your next class. Skip the dryer; they don’t need it, and heat can shorten their life.

If you remember one thing: rinse, air dry, repeat. Everything else is detail.

Travel + studio bag.

Toss them in your bag like any other shoe. They’re durable, they’re flexible, and they handle being packed against each other just fine. If you have time to air them out between classes, even better.

That’s really it.

Performance skins are meant to be worn, washed, and worn again — hundreds of times. Rinse after class, dry in the air, and they’ll feel exactly like they did on day one for class after class after class.

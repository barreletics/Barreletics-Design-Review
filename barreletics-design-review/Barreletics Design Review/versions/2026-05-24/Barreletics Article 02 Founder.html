Why we built a grip-sock replacement.

Eight years of barre, three failed prototypes, and the studio conversation that started Barreletics.

Brian S.Founder & head of product

Studio socks were never designed for the way modern Pilates, barre, and reformer practitioners actually move. We started Barreletics to build something honestly better.

The honest origin story: I’d been taking barre classes for years, and every six weeks I was buying another pair of grip socks. Silicone dots peeling off, fabric thinning, sweat absorbing everything. Even the premium brands. It was a category begging to be reinvented.

The three prototypes that didn’t work.

The first version tried to make a better sock. The second tried to make a hybrid shoe-sock. The third figured out that the answer wasn’t in the fabric world at all.

The studio conversation that changed it.

I was talking to a Megaformer instructor in Los Angeles who said something simple: “I don’t want a sock. I want a grip. I want my foot to feel like a foot.” That re-framed everything.

What we set out to do.

Build a single-material performance skin that grips like a tire, fits like a glove, rinses clean in a minute, and outlasts a year of dedicated studio use. Five years and 70+ iterations later — Barreletics.

Our story

Patented Performance Skins for barre, Pilates, reformer, Megaformer, and studio fitness. Built because grip socks kept failing when grip mattered most.

Stefanie Miller, Founder

We didn’t improve the grip sock. We made it obsolete.

The only tool available kept interrupting the work.

At the time, grippy yoga socks were the only option. They slipped when grip mattered most, required constant adjusting, and never delivered the control they promised.

I found myself breaking focus to fix my socks instead of holding the position — sliding out of side planks, resetting my feet, losing stability where it counted.

Fabric holds moisture. I wanted a clean, connected practice — not a sock working against it.

If I was dedicating an hour, I wanted that hour to count. Secure. Grounded. Not distracted by what was happening beneath my feet.

And I knew I was not the only one.

Stuck in side plank. Sliding. Again.

It became clear this was not inconvenient. It was holding us back.

Why didn’t a better tool exist? What if there were flexible, non-slip, in-studio footwear with true all-over grip — something that could move from side plank to flat-back chair without slipping, shifting, or interrupting flow?

That same evening, I tore the silicone sleeve off a water bottle, cut it up, and made the first prototype.

Far from perfect. But it worked.

Trial and error, until the grip never left.

That on-the-fly prototype became the starting point. What followed was two years of testing, redesigning, and refining the details that mattered: pressure points, fit, flexibility, material, true all-over grip.

After countless iterations, Performance Skins were born — patented studio footwear, designed and manufactured in Michigan.

Built to feel secure and connected. Built to stay out of the way of the workout.

The Pilates sock era is over. A better way to grip is here.

Joseph Pilates built the method around the feet.

Joseph Pilates called his method Contrology — breath, concentration, control, precision, alignment, flow, and full-body awareness. He designed the apparatus to serve that method: the Reformer, Cadillac, Wunda Chair, Ladder Barrel, Spine Corrector, Foot Corrector, Toe Corrector, and Ped-o-Pul.

The feet were always part of the system. In Pilates, the foot connects the body to the floor, the mat, the Reformer, the Cadillac, the Wunda Chair, the Megaformer — every surface beneath you. That connection is the work.

A sock alone does not make a practice clean. Fabric absorbs moisture and holds it. Clean feet, clean floors, wiped-down equipment — that’s what actually creates a clean environment. The tool under you should support the practice, not work against it.

Feedback is the practice.

Pilates depends on feedback. The foot has to sense, press, articulate, stabilize, and respond. A grip sock that slips or bunches moves your focus off form and onto your footing.

It is like holding a plank on a sliding towel. You can still do the move. Your body is compensating instead of engaging.

A practice built on control, precision, and connection should not be compromised by what is under your feet. That is the part Joseph Pilates — and any serious barre or Pilates practitioner — would understand immediately.

If you prefer a sock layer, have cold feet, or train in a studio that requires socks, Performance Skins can be worn over a very thin pair. The thinnest option possible, ideally toe-free or fitted, so you still feel the grip.

Barreletics, in brief

What is Barreletics?

Barreletics makes patented Performance Skins — studio footwear for barre, Pilates, reformer, Lagree, and Megaformer. Founded in Michigan by Stefanie Miller. One pair replaces disposable grip socks with 360° injection-molded grip.

Is this a grip sock alternative for Pilates?

Yes. Performance Skins are built as a grip sock alternative for Pilates, barre, and reformer — full-contact molded grip that does not peel the way silicone dots do. Same grip and stability in Open Sole or Closed Sole; choice is preference and feel only.

How does this relate to Joseph Pilates and Contrology?

Joseph Pilates designed Contrology around the body’s connection to the apparatus. The feet are part of that system — Reformer, Cadillac, Wunda Chair, mat. Performance Skins exist so that connection isn’t interrupted by a sock that slips or holds moisture.

Where are Performance Skins made?

Designed and manufactured in Michigan, USA. Material, injection molding, and quality stay domestic.

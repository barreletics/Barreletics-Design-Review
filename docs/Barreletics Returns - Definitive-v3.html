Returns

30-day returns

Initiate within 30 days of delivery for a refund when items are in new, sellable condition — clean, unused outdoors, no sole damage or scuffs.

Like buying any footwear: try them on for fit. This is not a studio trial and not “try them in class for 30 days.”

  • Performance Skins: tried on indoors only; return in the original mesh bag with tag (if supplied) and packing slip. Poly mailer OK — no shoe box required.
  • Apparel & accessories: unused, unwashed, tags attached, original packaging, packing slip.
  • US return shipping: $7.95 flat rate, deducted from refund. Prepaid label after approval.
  • Refunds: within 72 hours of inspection (bank processing may take longer).

Open the returns portal → or Contact with your order number.

Warranty

90-day warranty

Every purchase is covered against manufacturing defects for 90 days from delivery. Return of the defective item is usually not required.

Covered

  • Manufacturing defects
  • Structural failure from defect
  • Item arrives defective (contact within 24 hours)

Not covered

  • Normal wear and tear
  • Grip-surface wear from use
  • Damage from misuse or outdoor abuse

International: photo proof helps; customer pays replacement shipping + duties. File a claim →

Exchanges

Size exchanges

Wrong size? A size exchange is often faster than a refund. Start through the same returns portal within 30 days — select Exchange.

We ship your exchange at no cost to you once we receive and inspect the return. Include packing slip, order number, and your name in the package.

Not sure on size? Size chart · FAQ → Fit · Contact before you reorder.

International

International orders & returns

For international shipments, customers are responsible for outbound shipping, return shipping, and any duties, taxes, or customs fees. Size carefully before ordering — return and exchange shipping costs are the customer’s responsibility.

Warranty replacements: customer pays associated shipping and duties. We’ll process in a way that minimizes extra charges when we can — duties remain outside our control.

Lost packages: once tracking confirms delivery to your address, the order is considered fulfilled. Check building/mail areas first. Questions? Contact us.

How to start a return

Three steps. Keep packaging if you can — condition still has to be sellable.

01

Open the portal

Visit the returns portal within 30 days of delivery. Select Refund or Exchange and follow the prompts.

02

Pack & ship

Skins go in the mesh bag + packing slip, poly mailer OK. Use the prepaid US label ($7.95 deducted from refund). Items must arrive in new, sellable condition.

03

Refund or exchange

Once inspected, we process your refund (typically within 72 hours) or send the exchange size. Defect claims under warranty — Contact us first; return often not required.

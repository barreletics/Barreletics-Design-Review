FAQ

Everything you need to know.

Tired of Pilates socks and yoga socks that slip?
Pilates socks, yoga socks, and barre grip socks fail at the dots — they peel, stretch, and force resets in chair pose and flat back chair. Barreletics Performance Skins are grippy shoes with 360° full-contact grip: not patches, not another disposable sock. Secure in Every Hold for barre, Pilates, yoga, reformer, Megaformer, Lagree, and hot studios.
Are these good for reformer Pilates, Megaformer, Lagree & hot Pilates?
Yes — engineered for reformer carriage load, Megaformer, Lagree, barre, hot Pilates, and yoga. The 360° grip handles lateral forces that destroy traditional Pilates socks and yoga socks. Closed Sole for full lockdown; Open Sole when you want heel freedom.
How long do they actually last?
Customers report 18+ months with daily studio use and zero grip degradation. The patented material doesn't wear down like silicone dots or rubber patches. One pair replaces 6–8 pairs of grip socks per year.
How do I clean them?
Warm soapy water, rinse, air dry. Ready for your next class in under an hour. Never machine wash or bleach. The grip lasts indefinitely with proper care.
What size should I order?
Available now: M (Women 5–7.5, Men 6–8) and L (Women 8–10, Men 8.5–11). Small is coming soon (planned ~Women 4–5 / Men 5–6). The stretch-fit material conforms to your foot — if you're between sizes, go larger for the most comfortable fit.
Open Sole vs Closed Sole — which one?
Closed Sole: full underfoot coverage, ideal for reformer and Megaformer where you need maximum contact area. Open Sole: breathable toe freedom, preferred for barre and yoga. Same patented grip system in both builds.
What's the return and warranty policy?
30-day returns on items in new, sellable condition — try on for fit at home. 90-day warranty covers manufacturing defects only. Free shipping on orders over $150. We ship within 1–2 business days.

Product 10

What’s the difference between Closed Sole and Open Sole?

Closed Sole covers the entire foot including the heel — preferred for reformer footwork series, Cadillac push-through and parakeet, Megaformer Super Lunge, and Lagree machine work. Also doubles as our outdoor / water build.

Open Sole leaves the heel open for a barefoot feel on mat Pilates, yoga (chair pose, warrior, downward dog), and hot Pilates.

Same patented 360° grip, same $74 — pick the build for your practice. Shop the collection.

Why are Barreletics better than grip socks?

Grip socks have two failure points: silicone/latex dots that peel, and fabric that slips on your foot. Performance Skins use full-bottom structured grip — locked-in through holds, transitions, and balance. No sliding. No resets.

  • Sweat-resistant — stays fresh through intense sessions
  • Latex- and silicone-free
  • Natural toe splay for balance and control
  • Second-skin fit — no stretching out every few weeks

One pair. No more socks.

Will these hold on reformer, Cadillac, Megaformer, Lagree, and barre?

Yes. Built for barre (plié, relevé, flat back chair, water ski), Classical and reformer Pilates (footwork series, elephant, knee stretches, long stretch series), Cadillac / trapeze table (push-through, parakeet, leg spring series), Chair, Tower, Megaformer and Lagree (Super Lunge, Catfish, Bear, Plank to Pike), yoga, hot yoga, and hot Pilates.

Full-contact grip — not silicone dots. For heated rooms, wear a thin sock or liner under the Skin to absorb sweat; the Skin stays the traction layer.

Are they latex- and silicone-free? Hygienic?

Yes. No latex, no silicone. Antimicrobial, sweat-resistant material that doesn’t hold moisture or bacteria the way fabric grip socks do — cleaner on shared reformer carriages and Megaformers. Made in USA. Trusted by 1,000’s of instructors and studios.

Can I wear them barefoot or with a thin sock? (Hot Pilates)

Either works. Many prefer the barefoot feel. Others use a thin toeless or individual-toe sock for warmth or moisture. Skip thick or full-toe socks — they reduce natural toe splay.

Hot Pilates / heated rooms: a thin full sock or liner under the Skin absorbs sweat like a towel on the mat; grip and performance stay with the Skin. Hot Pilates & Yoga Kits (Skin + liner) are coming.

Studio requires socks? Thin individual-toe or thin full sock underneath keeps you compliant while outperforming grip socks alone.

My studio requires socks — can I still wear Barreletics?

Yes. Wear a thin individual-toe or thin full sock underneath the Performance Skin. You stay studio-compliant; grip and performance stay with the Skin — not the sock.

Will they make my feet hot or sweaty?

Usually the opposite — open-toe design and lightweight material circulate air better than a full fabric sock. Material is sweat-resistant by design.

In hot yoga / hot Pilates, extra sweat collects at the feet — a thin sock underneath absorbs moisture without killing grip.

Will they grip on carpet, studio floors, and wet surfaces?

Studio surfaces — exceptional: hardwood, rubber, vinyl, carpet, reformer carriages, Megaformers, yoga mats.

Inherently slippery surfaces — no footwear wins: wet polished marble, certain wet tiles, oiled/waxed floors, wet stone. Exercise caution — Barreletics outperform bare feet in most cases, but they can’t invent traction where the surface has none.

Can I wear them outdoors, at the beach, or on a boat?

Closed Sole especially works beyond the studio — beach, paddleboarding, outdoor yoga, boat decks, resortwear, hot sand — grip and ground feel without shoe bulk. Not a replacement for heavy-duty footwear in extreme conditions.

Same caution on inherently slippery wet tile/spa surfaces applies outdoors.

What else are they good for besides studio?

Weight lifting, outdoor volleyball, martial arts (Tai Chi, Qi Gong, Karate), barefoot running, boating, beach, rock climbing for barefoot enthusiasts — anywhere you’d go barefoot but want grip and protection.

Fit & sizing 4

What size should I order?

Start with your regular shoe size. Performance Skins fit like sock sizing:

  • M — Women’s 5.5–7.5 (Kids 2–5)
  • L — Women’s 7.5–11 · Men’s up to 10.5

M and L ship today; Small is coming soon. Between 7.5 and 8? Wider foot → Large. Narrower → Medium. Between sizes? Most studio practitioners size up for comfort.

Open the size chart →

They feel too tight or causing toe pressure

Usually means size up one. A size exchange is often faster than a refund — start at the returns portal or Contact us.

Dark Grey, Hot Coral, Dark Shadow, and Blue run snugger than Light Grey / Black (more forgiving).

They feel too loose or shift during movement

Usually means size down. If you’re already in the smallest size, a thin toeless or individual-toe sock underneath fine-tunes fit while keeping grip.

Ordered Light Grey and it’s loose? Try Dark Grey, Hot Coral, or Blue for a snugger natural fit — or exchange via the returns portal.

I have narrower feet

Many narrow-footed customers wear a thin toeless or individual-toe sock underneath for security. Or choose Dark Grey, Hot Coral, or Blue — they fit slightly tighter than Light Grey.

Not sure? Contact us for a personal recommendation before you order.

How do I put them on?

Pull from the top of the foot — not the straps. Pulling the straps stresses the material and shortens lifespan.

Care & durability 2

How do I clean Performance Skins?

After class: when you wipe equipment, give the Skins a quick wipe with the same cloth — takes seconds.

Regular clean: warm soapy water, rinse thoroughly, air dry. Never machine wash or bleach. Ready for the next class in under an hour.

Thin socks worn underneath for hot Pilates: wash those as you would any sock. See the Journal for care stories.

How long do they last?

Built in the USA. Many report 18+ months; some wear the same pair 6+ days/week for 3+ years with zero issues. Never loses shape. Never loses grip. Same performance on class 1 as on class 1,000 when cared for.

One pair replaces 6–8 pairs of traditional grip socks per year (illustrative sock-cost ranges).

Pro tip: pull from the top of the foot when putting them on — not the straps.

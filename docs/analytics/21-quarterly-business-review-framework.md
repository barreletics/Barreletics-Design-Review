# 21 — Quarterly Business Review Framework

**Purpose:** Strategic review of category creation, unit economics, and resource allocation.  
**Audience:** Founder; advisors / future executives.  
**Related:** [02](./02-north-star-metrics.md), [20](./20-monthly-executive-review-process.md), [22](./22-annual-planning-framework.md).

---

## Cadence

| Item | Value |
|------|-------|
| Frequency | Once per fiscal/calendar quarter |
| Duration | Half-day (or 2× 90 min) |
| Pack owner | Founder with Marketing/Ops inputs |
| Horizon | Look back 1 quarter; look forward 1–2 quarters |

# 20 — Monthly Executive Review Process

**Purpose:** Ritual to review business health and make resource decisions.  
**Audience:** Founder (chair); Marketing, Ops, CS, Wholesale as available.  
**Related:** [01](./01-executive-kpi-dashboard.md), [21](./21-quarterly-business-review-framework.md), [25](./25-reporting-sop.md).  
**OS link:** Align with Operating System monthly watches (by name).

---

## Cadence & ownership

| Item | Value |
|------|-------|
| Frequency | Once per calendar month (first week of following month preferred) |
| Duration | 60–90 minutes |
| Pack owner | Marketing (or Founder if solo) |
| Decision owner | Founder |
| Inputs due | T−2 business days |

---

## Pre-read pack (required)

1. Executive KPI summary ([01](./01-executive-kpi-dashboard.md)) — MoM  
2. Attribution reconciliation — Shopify vs Meta vs GA4 ([04](./04-marketing-attribution-framework.md))  
3. Profitability proxy ([12](./12-profitability-dashboard.md))  
4. Funnel health ([16](./16-conversion-funnel-dashboard.md))  
5. Inventory criticals ([13](./13-inventory-dashboard.md))  
6. Top products ([14](./14-product-performance-dashboard.md))  
7. Wholesale / studio / ambassador highlights ([17](./17-wholesale-kpi-dashboard.md)–[19](./19-studio-kpi-dashboard.md))  
8. North star notes ([02](./02-north-star-metrics.md))  

---

## Agenda

| Time | Topic | KPI focus | Owner |
|------|-------|-----------|-------|
| 0:00 | Scoreboard | Net sales, orders, contribution after ads | Founder |
| 0:15 | Acquisition | Spend, Shopify ROAS, CAC, overclaim gap | Marketing |
| 0:30 | Funnel & site | ATC/checkout, channel rev/session | Marketing |
| 0:40 | Product & inventory | Heroes, OOS, cover | Ops |
| 0:50 | Retention | Cohort RPR, email | Marketing |
| 1:00 | Partnerships | Wholesale/studio/ambassador | Partnerships |
| 1:10 | Decisions | Budget, creative, ops | Founder |
| 1:25 | Actions log | Owners + due dates | All |

---

## Decision checklist (Founder)

| Decision | Inputs |
|----------|--------|
| Scale / hold / cut Meta (and other paid) | Shopify Meta ROAS, contribution, inventory |
| Promo intensity | Margin dilution, AOV vs $150 ship threshold |
| Production / reorder | Velocity + cover ([13](./13-inventory-dashboard.md)) |
| GEO / content bets | Order concentration + studio density |
| Partnership focus | Pipeline quality not vanity counts |

---

## KPI minimum set for the meeting

Each with Definition / Formula / Source / Owner / Cadence / Target as defined in linked docs:

- Net sales, Orders, AOV  
- Contribution after ads  
- Blended CAC + LTV:CAC (if month closed for windows)  
- ROAS_Shopify_Meta + Meta overclaim ratio  
- Channel rev/session vs benchmarks  
- Critical stockouts  
- Refund rate  
- Active studio / wholesale notes  

---

## Output artifacts

1. **Decisions log** — date, decision, rationale (can mirror `planning/10-decision-log.md` style for strategic items)  
2. **Action list** — owner, due date  
3. **Target updates** — if any KPI exits TBD baseline status → [24](./24-metric-governance.md)

We didn’t improve the grip sock. We made it obsolete.

Our story

Patented Performance Skins for barre, Pilates, reformer, Megaformer, and studio fitness. Built because grip socks kept failing when grip mattered most.

Stefanie Miller, Founder

Fashion trained the eye. The studio set the standard.

A former career as a fashion model took me around the world — design, textiles, architecture, craftsmanship, the details that elevate an experience.

That foundation meets a practice I take seriously. At the time, grippy yoga socks were the only tool available. They slipped when grip mattered most, required constant adjusting, and never delivered the control they promised.

I found myself breaking focus to fix my socks instead of holding the position — sliding out of side planks, resetting my feet. Fabric holds moisture. I wanted a clean, connected practice.

If I was dedicating an hour to my practice, I wanted that hour to count — secure, grounded, connected, and confident. And I knew I was not the only one.

The idea became the product.

One day, stuck in side plank and sliding yet again, it became clear: this was not just inconvenient. It was holding us back.

That same evening, I tore the silicone sleeve off a water bottle, cut it up, and made the first prototype. Far from perfect. But it worked.

Two years of testing followed — pressure points, fit, flexibility, material, true all-over grip. Performance Skins were born: patented studio footwear, designed and manufactured in the USA. Built to feel secure and connected. Built to stay out of the way of the workout.

Joseph Pilates built the method around the feet.

Joseph Pilates developed his method, originally called Contrology, around breath, concentration, control, precision, alignment, flow, and full-body awareness. He also created the apparatus to serve that method: the Reformer, Cadillac, Wunda Chair, Ladder Barrel, Spine Corrector, Foot Corrector, Toe Corrector, and Ped-o-Pul.

The feet were always part of the system. In Pilates, the foot connects the body to the floor, the mat, the Reformer, the Cadillac, the Wunda Chair, the Megaformer — every surface beneath you. That connection is the work.

Pilates depends on feedback. The foot has to sense, press, articulate, stabilize, and respond. A grip sock that slips, shifts, or bunches moves your focus off form and onto your footing. It is like holding a plank on a sliding towel. You can still do the move. Your body is compensating instead of engaging.

Clean feet, clean floors, wiped-down equipment — that’s what creates a clean environment. The tool under you should support the practice, not work against it.

If you prefer a sock layer, have cold feet, or train in a studio that requires socks, Performance Skins can be worn over a very thin pair — toe-free or fitted — so you still feel the grip.

Barreletics, in brief

What is Barreletics?

Barreletics makes patented Performance Skins — studio footwear for barre, Pilates, reformer, Lagree, and Megaformer. Founded in Michigan by Stefanie Miller. Made in the USA. One pair replaces disposable grip socks with 360° injection-molded grip.

Open Sole or Closed Sole?

Closed Sole is heel and foot fully covered. Open Sole is heel exposed, mid-foot breathing hole — more grounded, barefoot feel, natural toe splay. Both perform identically — same grip, same stability. Choice is preference and feel only.

How does this relate to Joseph Pilates and Contrology?

Joseph Pilates designed Contrology around the body’s connection to the apparatus. The feet are part of that system — Reformer, Cadillac, Wunda Chair, mat. Performance Skins exist so that connection isn’t interrupted by a sock that slips or holds moisture.

Where are Performance Skins made?

Made in the USA. Designed and manufactured in Michigan. Material, injection molding, and quality stay domestic.

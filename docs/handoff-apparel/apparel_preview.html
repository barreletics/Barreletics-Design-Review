Questions from the Studio

How should I size the high-rise yoga pants?
Size down. Super-high rise Italian 4-way stretch compression runs large. Use the size chart for waist, hip, and inseam. Most studio athletes drop one size for a locked-in hold.
Are the knees padded or reinforced?
Reinforced, not bulky pads. Built-up knee panels support kneeling, transitions, and floor work in barre, Pilates, and yoga without looking like gear.
Will the tees and pants pill?
No. Performance fabric tees and reinforced-knee pants are built to stay smooth class after class, no pilling, no bagging out.
What workouts are they built for?
Barre, mat Pilates, reformer, Classical apparatus, Megaformer, Lagree, yoga, and hot yoga. Compression pants for holds and floor work; soft performance tees for breathable layers.
How do I wash Barreletics apparel?
Machine wash cold, delicate, inside out. Lay flat to dry. Skip high heat. It kills compression and stretch.
What's your return policy on apparel?
30-day returns on apparel unused, unworn, unwashed, with tags and original packaging. Free shipping on orders over $150. Ships in 1-2 days. Apparel does not include a warranty.

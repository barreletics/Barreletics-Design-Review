Our story

Patented Performance Skins for barre, Pilates, reformer, Megaformer, and studio fitness. Built because grip socks kept failing when grip mattered most.

Stefanie Miller, Founder

We didn’t improve the grip sock. We made it obsolete.

The only tool available kept interrupting the work.

Grippy yoga socks were the only option. They slipped when grip mattered most. I kept breaking focus to fix my socks instead of holding the position.

Fabric holds moisture. I wanted a clean, connected practice.

That same evening, I tore the silicone sleeve off a water bottle, cut it up, and made the first prototype. Two years of testing followed.

Performance Skins were born: patented studio footwear, designed and manufactured in the USA.

Joseph Pilates, Contrology, and the apparatus

Joseph Pilates developed his method, originally called Contrology, around breath, concentration, control, precision, alignment, flow, and full-body awareness. He also created the apparatus to serve that method: the Reformer, Cadillac, Wunda Chair, Ladder Barrel, Spine Corrector, Foot Corrector, Toe Corrector, and Ped-o-Pul.

The feet were always part of the system. In Pilates, the foot connects the body to the floor, the mat, the Reformer, the Cadillac, the Wunda Chair, the Megaformer — every surface beneath you.

Pilates depends on feedback. The foot has to sense, press, articulate, stabilize, and respond. A grip sock that slips, shifts, or bunches moves your focus off form and onto your footing. It is like holding a plank on a sliding towel. You can still do the move. Your body is compensating instead of engaging.

That is the part Joseph Pilates — and any serious barre or Pilates practitioner — would understand immediately. A practice built on control, precision, and connection should not be compromised by what is under your feet.

Clean feet, clean floors, wiped-down equipment — that’s what creates a clean environment. The tool under you should support the practice, not work against it.

If you prefer a sock layer, have cold feet, or train in a studio that requires socks, Performance Skins can be worn over a very thin pair. The thinnest option possible, ideally toe-free or fitted, so you still feel the grip.

Barreletics, in brief

What is Barreletics?

Barreletics makes patented Performance Skins — studio footwear for barre, Pilates, reformer, Lagree, and Megaformer. Founded in Michigan by Stefanie Miller. Made in the USA. One pair replaces disposable grip socks with 360° injection-molded grip.

Open Sole or Closed Sole?

Closed Sole is heel and foot fully covered. Open Sole is heel exposed, mid-foot breathing hole — more grounded, barefoot feel, natural toe splay. Both perform identically — same grip, same stability. Choice is preference and feel only.

Is this a grip sock alternative for Pilates?

Yes. Performance Skins are built as a grip sock alternative for Pilates, barre, and reformer — full-contact molded grip that does not peel the way silicone dots do.

Where are Performance Skins made?

Made in the USA. Designed and manufactured in Michigan. Material, injection molding, and quality stay domestic.

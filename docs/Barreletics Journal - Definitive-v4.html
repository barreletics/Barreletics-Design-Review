Questions from the studio

Pilates socks, yoga socks, machines, and heated rooms — one place. Articles above stay fully dynamic from Shopify.

Tired of Pilates socks and yoga socks that slip in class?
Most Pilates socks, yoga socks, and barre grip socks fail the same way: silicone dots peel, fabric stretches, and you reset in chair pose or flat back chair. Barreletics Performance Skins are grippy shoes — full-contact 360° grip. Not another disposable sock. Secure in Every Hold for barre, Pilates, yoga, and every apparatus below.
Are these a replacement for barre grip socks and studio socks?
Yes — that's the job. Traditional barre grip socks and Pilates socks absorb sweat and lose traction in weeks. One Performance Skin pair (Closed Sole or Open Sole, $74) holds through footwork, water ski, lunges, and heated rooms without the sock-replacement cycle.
Which machines and disciplines are they built for?
Barre, mat Pilates, Classical Pilates, reformer, Cadillac (trapeze table), Chair, Tower, Megaformer, Lagree, yoga, and hot yoga. Same 360° grip whether you're on the carriage, holding the Cadillac bar (parakeet / push-through), digging into Super Lunges on Megaformer, or flowing on the mat. Closed Sole for full-foot lockdown on machines; Open Sole when you want heel freedom on mat and yoga days.
What about hot Pilates and heated studios?
Yes — for hot Pilates, hot yoga, and humid rooms. The Skin is the traction layer; silicone-dot socks fail when rooms run hot. For hot Pilates and other heated classes, we recommend wearing a thin sock (or thin liner) under the Performance Skin to absorb sweat — keep feet comfortable while the grip stays locked to the floor or carriage. Hot Pilates & Yoga Kits (Skin + thin liner) are coming; until then, any thin, low-bulk sock works under the Skin.
Reformer, Cadillac, Megaformer & Lagree — will they hold?
Carriage load and lateral force shred barre grip socks. Instructors trust Closed Sole on reformer, Cadillac, Chair, Tower, Megaformer, and Lagree for full underfoot contact; Open Sole for mat, yoga, and when you want a barefoot heel. Grip that holds the bar on Cadillac work — not a sock that slides off mid-exercise.
Best alternative if I'm searching for better Pilates or yoga socks?
If search brought you here for Pilates socks or yoga socks that last, the upgrade is a Performance Skin: full underfoot grip instead of dots. Built for barre, Classical and reformer Pilates, Cadillac, Megaformer, Lagree, yoga, and hot Pilates — no slipping, no resets.
How do I wash Performance Skins?
Wipe with water or mild soap. Non-porous grip doesn't hold sweat like fabric Pilates socks or yoga socks — rinse, dry, reuse. Thin socks/liners worn underneath for hot Pilates: wash those as you would any sock. Full care guides live in the articles above.

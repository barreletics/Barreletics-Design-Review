## 3. Direct pitching to editors and newsletters

2. Product problem story: "Why grip socks slip, bunch and wear out, and what studios use instead." Pitch to wellness and fitness service editors. Offer Stefanie as the expert source.

4. Gift angle: under $100, one pair for any Pilates, barre or reformer regular. Pitch to gift guide writers (section 4).
5. Local angle for Detroit and Michigan: designed and made in Michigan (confirm wording).

### Subject line formula
`[Their section or guide]: [product in plain words] for [reader], [one hard fact]`

Examples (no dashes):
- `Gift guide idea: grip shoes for Pilates and barre, $74, made in Michigan`
- `Story idea: why grip socks slip, and what a Paris runway label picked instead`
- `For your Pilates gear list: Barreletics Performance Skin, sample ready`

Rules from editors: Well+Good's Erica Sloan wants a timely hook, a clear wellness reason, and one clear ask, one client per email, with plain subject lines (OnePitch interview, SECONDARY). Keep pitches under about 200 words. One follow up with new info only.

## 7. Blog (Barreletics Journal) distribution

1. **Google Search Console** (free). Shopify auto generates sitemap.xml with blog posts. Verify the domain, then Indexing > Sitemaps > submit `sitemap.xml`. Steps VERIFIED on https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/promoting-marketing/seo/find-site-map . Google does not guarantee timing. Check whether it is already done (Andrew, 5 minutes).
2. **Bing Webmaster Tools** (free). Import the site from Google Search Console, then submit `https://barreletics.com/sitemap.xml` (SECONDARY, Bing help page and a Shopify guide). IndexNow is a free Bing protocol for pushing changed URLs; whether Shopify needs an app for it is not confirmed. Optional.
3. **Medium.** Use "Import a story" with the original URL. Medium backdates the post and adds a canonical link to the original (VERIFIED, Medium Help Center). Only import posts Barreletics owns. Do not import ITSLIQUID or INTERNI copy.
4. **LinkedIn.** No canonical tag support (SECONDARY). Post a short teaser with the link, not the full article.
5. **Substack.** No per post canonical field documented (SECONDARY). If used, write an original newsletter edition that summarizes a post and links to it. Do not paste full duplicates.
6. **Pinterest.** Idea Pins were folded into standard Pins in 2023 (SECONDARY), so make normal Pins linking to each Journal post. Install Pinterest for Shopify, which claims the site. Article Rich Pins read page metadata and the old validator and approval step no longer exists (VERIFIED, Shopify blog Sep 22, 2026). Best Journal posts for Pins: "7 Barre and Pilates Technique Mistakes", "Why Grip Socks Fall Short". Make 2 or 3 vertical images per post.
7. **Instagram and Facebook.** Stefanie already posts organic. Add UTM tagged links (utm_source=instagram or facebook, utm_medium=organic_social, utm_campaign=journal_post_slug) in bio, Stories and FB posts.
8. **Email newsletter.** Add the latest Journal post as one block in the next customer email. The email tool was not identified in this research.
9. **Fix the Journal itself:** several posts are summaries of outside features (ITSLIQUID, INTERNI). Add a "Press" page on the site listing every feature with logo, date and link, and make sure each Journal post has a unique title, meta description and image alt text.

## 8. Safe link building (no paid links, no spam directories)

- **Reclaim links from existing coverage.** Search for ITSLIQUID, INTERNI, Anthropologie, Free People, Coperni and other mentions. If a mention does not link to barreletics.com, ask the editor for a plain link. A bot can find these. A human sends the note.
- **Retailer and partner pages.** Anthropologie and Free People product pages and brand pages should link back. Ask the brand contact.
- **Studio and instructor pages.** Studios that sell or recommend Performance Skin can link from "what to bring" or "shop" pages. Offer a short studio page on barreletics.com listing partner studios with links, which encourages reciprocal mention. Keep it real: only studios that actually partner.
- **Instructor program.** Instructors link from their bios and class pages. Use codes and UTMs.
- **Resource pages.** Search for "what to wear to Pilates", "barre class gear", "reformer class checklist" pages by studios and bloggers. Send a short note offering a useful fact or photo, not a link request.
- **Journalist query quotes** (section 2). Links are usually earned here.
- **Brand directories:** only reputable ones tied to real facts: Michigan made or Detroit small business listings, women owned business certifications, the local chamber. I did not verify specific directories. Skip any "submit to 200 directories" service.
- **Do not:** buy links, trade product for dofollow links in bulk, or use link exchange schemes. If product is gifted to a reviewer, the link should be marked rel=sponsored or nofollow and the review disclosed (Google's link spam guidance, general principle, not re-fetched in this run).

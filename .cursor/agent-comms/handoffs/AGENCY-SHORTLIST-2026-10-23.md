# Agency & Specialist Shortlist: Small DTC Brand (~$7k/mo Meta spend)

**Prepared:** Fri 2026-10-23 (America/Detroit) · **Method:** WebSearch + WebFetch of each agency's own pages only. **No one was contacted.**
**Target budget:** about $2k–$6k/mo total agency fees (project fees OK). **Categories:** apparel, footwear, fitness, wellness, accessories.

## How to read this
- **Proof found** = what I saw on the agency's own site. Almost all of it is **self-reported and not independently verified**. I did not confirm any client relationship with the client brand.
- Tags: **[PUBLISHED]** price is on their site. **[THIRD-PARTY]** price is only from someone else's listicle, so treat it as unverified. **[NOT PUBLISHED]** no price found.
- **Fit score (1–10)** weighs: category fit, price fit, strength of verifiable proof, and contract flexibility. It is a judgment call, not a measurement.
- Team size is rarely published. "Not stated" means I did not find it.
- Several sources are SEO-style pages or competitor "best of" listicles. Treat any number without a named brand and a dashboard as a claim, not proof.

## Vetting checklist (before any call becomes a contract)
1. **Brand ownership:** your Business Manager owns the ad account, pixel, domain, Klaviyo, Shopify. The agency gets **partner/admin access you can revoke**. Never let them run ads from their account.
2. **Named references:** ask for 2 clients of similar size (spend $5k–$15k/mo, apparel/fitness) you can email. Ask the reference what changed and what they'd do differently.
3. **Dashboard proof:** ask to see the same account screenshots (Meta, Klaviyo, Triple Whale) for the case studies they cite, with date ranges and spend. Compare to what's on their site.
4. **Who does the work:** names, roles and capacity of the person on your account. Ask how many accounts that person runs. (Raintree's 8–15 accounts per founder is the type of thing to ask.)
5. **Scope in writing:** deliverables per month (ads, flows, creators seeded, pitches sent), not outcomes.
6. **Pass-through costs:** who pays creators, product, shipping, SMS credits, Klaviyo plan, ad spend (no markup).
7. **Contract:** month to month with 30-day notice, or a defined paid pilot. Clear exit and asset handover.
8. **Reporting:** a baseline you agree on first (MER, new customer CAC, return rate, email share of revenue).
9. **Content rights:** perpetual usage rights, and whitelisting rights for creators.
10. **FTC disclosure:** agency writes #ad/partnership disclosures into every creator brief.
11. **Cross-check:** Meta Ad Library for the agency's claimed clients, LinkedIn for team size, Clutch/Trustpilot for complaints. Check domain age.

## Red flags
- **Guaranteed ROAS, revenue or placement outcomes.** (Exception: guarantees of effort, like KoLabs' 100 creators accepted or INDIGO's extra month, are fine if you read the fine print.)
- **Agency-owned ad account**, pixel or Klaviyo, or no handover of assets on exit.
- **Contracts longer than 3 months**, auto-renewal, or setup fees that lock you in. Seen: Laylush, Everframe, PELORA (3-month minimums), TAT PR (6 months), GetDigitize (6 months), Push The Envelope (6 recommended).
- **Anonymous case studies** ("a DTC apparel brand"), or case studies labeled "prior agency work".
- **Percent-of-spend fees** that grow when your spend does, or hidden media markup.
- **Vanity metrics** (impressions, views, EMV) used as results.
- **Founder runs 10+ accounts** with no named delegates.
- **Blanket "views" guarantees** and "thousands of DMs" volume pitches with no vetting.
- **Reviews only on the agency's own site** or on freelancer platforms.
- **Claims that contradict each other** on the same page (Raintree).

## 30-day paid trial structure (use for each)
**Before day 0 (1 week)**
- Written 30-day SOW. Fixed fee. **No auto-renewal.** Exit on 30 days' notice or earlier.
- Baseline report from you: last 90 days of MER, new-customer CAC, AOV, return rate, email/SMS share of revenue, creators posting rate.
- Access: partner or limited-admin roles on your accounts. Agency reports from your data.
- Write the kill criteria and the **activity** commitments below. Do not accept ROAS guarantees, and do not offer any.

**Week 1:** audit and plan delivered. Tracking check (pixel, CAPI, Klaviyo attribution). You approve the plan.
**Week 2:** first deliverables live.
**Week 3:** first read, with changes made from it.
**Week 4:** end-of-trial review against baseline, then go/no-go. Keep decision rights with you.

| Vendor | Trial deliverables (activity) | What you judge on |
|---|---|---|
| KoLabs | first creators seeded within 14 days (their claim); 100+ accepted collabs by day 60 (their guarantee) | acceptance rate, posts live, usable ad clips, cost per usable asset |
| Inbox Copy | audit, 8 core flows live (their 45-day claim, so ask for 30 days), calendar of 3–4 sends/week | flow revenue per recipient, email share of revenue vs baseline, deliverability |
| Raintree | 2-week "Reset" audit, restructure by week 3, bi-weekly creative batch | new-customer CAC and MER vs baseline; creative volume actually shipped |
| Blue Forest | audit, structure rebuild, first test cycle | same, plus quality of their measurement approach |
| Fair PR | positioning session, media list, first pitches, samples requested | pitches sent, journalist replies, sample requests. **Not coverage** in 30 days |

**Go/no-go:** continue only if (a) deliverables arrived on time, (b) reporting matches your own dashboards, (c) at least one leading metric moved in the right direction, and (d) you can describe what they will do in month two.

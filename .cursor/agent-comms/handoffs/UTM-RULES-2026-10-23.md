## Verdict
**Product tags alone are not enough.** Shopify's Facebook & Instagram channel is connected, but in the last 30 days only 1 of 224 orders carried the product-tag marker, and 0 organic Instagram/Facebook orders carried a campaign tag. You cannot tie an order to a post without UTM links plus a dated post log.

## UTM rules for Stefanie (organic)
Always lowercase, no spaces. Campaign = `yyyymmdd-short-name` (the post date). Log each post's date, link and format in the post sheet (S4).
1. **Base:** `https://barreletics.com/<page>?utm_source=instagram|facebook&utm_medium=organic_social&utm_campaign=<yyyymmdd-short-name>&utm_content=<post-type>`
2. **Bio link (Instagram):** `https://barreletics.com/collections/barre-pilates-yoga-shoe-sock-footwear?utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=organic_social&utm_campaign=20261026-bio&utm_content=bio-link`. It replaces today's ig/social/link_in_bio link. When a post goes live, swap the campaign to that post's date and name.
3. **Story link sticker:** `...?utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=organic_social&utm_campaign=20261028-hear-the-grip&utm_content=story-link`
4. **Reel or feed post (Instagram):** captions are not clickable links, so write "link in bio" and set the bio link to that post's campaign, `utm_content=reel-bio` or `post-bio`.
5. **Facebook post caption:** `https://barreletics.com/products/best-reformer-pilates-legree-workout-shoes?utm_source=facebook&utm_medium=organic_social&utm_campaign=20261028-hear-the-grip&utm_content=fb-post`
6. **Product tag:** still tag products. You cannot add UTMs to a tag (Meta adds IGShopping / Social itself). The post log is the only way to tie those orders to a post.
7. **ManyChat link:** `...?utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=organic_social&utm_campaign=20261028-<keyword>&utm_content=manychat-<keyword>`
8. **Check:** tap each link on your phone and confirm the tags show in the address bar. A tagged order shows in Shopify under the campaign name within a day.

## Zaki (paid ads), already working, do not change
Meta ad level, "URL parameters" field, on every new or duplicated ad:
`utm_source={{site_source_name}}&utm_medium=paid_social&utm_campaign={{campaign.name}}&utm_content={{ad.name}}&utm_term={{adset.name}}`
Never use utm_medium=organic_social on an ad. Keep ad names unique, and don't rename a live ad (the name is the join key in reports). Check one order per new ad within a week.

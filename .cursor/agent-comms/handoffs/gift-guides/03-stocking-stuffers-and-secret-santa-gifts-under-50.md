<h3>1. Performance Fabric Tank Top, $34</h3>
<p>The <a href="/products/barreletics-performance-fabric-yoga-t-shirts">Performance Fabric Tank Top</a> is a lightweight, loose fit top for barre, Pilates and yoga. It is $34. Sizes are limited in some cases, so check stock early.</p>

<p>The tee is the same product in a V neck style and costs $39. Both can be returned within 30 days if they are unworn, unwashed and still have their tags. Use the <a href="/pages/yoga-pants-t-shirt-size-guide">apparel size guide</a> if you are guessing at a size.</p>
<h3>A note on shipping</h3>
<p>Orders over $150 ship free in the continental US. Smaller orders have a flat rate of $9.95, so a $34 tank is $43.95 and a $39 tee is $48.95, before tax. Both stay under $50. If your Secret Santa limit includes shipping, that is worth knowing.</p>
<h3>3. A group gift for a pair of grip shoes, about $37 each for two</h3>
<p>A pair of <a href="/collections/barre-pilates-yoga-shoe-sock-footwear">Barreletics Performance Skin grip shoes</a> is $74 for most colors, so it does not fit under $50 on its own. But two people can split it at $37 each, and four can split it at $18.50 each. This works well for a family gift, an office swap or a group of friends who go to class together. Check the <a href="/pages/performance-skins-size-chart">size chart</a> first, since a pair has to fit the person who will wear it.</p>
<h2>More ideas under $50</h2>
<p>These are general ideas with no brand attached. As a rough guide, plan to spend $10 to $25 on a stocking stuffer and $25 to $50 on a Secret Santa gift. Look at the price at checkout, since it will vary.</p>
<h3>4. Hair clips or ties that stay put</h3>
<p>Claw clips and soft ties are used every class and lost every month. A small set is easy to wrap and hard to get wrong.</p>
<h3>5. A small massage ball</h3>
<p>A firm ball for feet and shoulders is cheap and gets used for years. Skip it for anyone with an injury, and let them decide how hard to roll.</p>
<h3>6. Resistance bands or a Pilates ball</h3>
<p>Light bands and a small soft ball are good for home work between classes. They also fit in a stocking.</p>
<h3>7. A quick dry towel</h3>
<p>A small towel that folds flat is one of the most used items in any gym bag. Pick a plain color so it works for any taste.</p>
<h3>8. A water bottle</h3>
<p>Choose a size that fits a cup holder and a bag pocket. A bottle is a safe pick for a coworker, since it does not need a size.</p>
<h3>9. A class drop in at their studio</h3>
<p>If your budget is $50, one drop in class is often within range, depending on the studio. Check the price at the front desk or on their site, and ask whether the class pass can be gifted by email.</p>
<h2>Stocking stuffer or Secret Santa: what is the difference?</h2>
<p>A stocking stuffer is small and personal, so you can be specific about their habits. A Secret Santa gift often goes to someone you see at work or in class, so something safe and useful works better. If you are buying for both, start with the $34 tank for someone you know well, and a towel or a bottle for someone you do not.</p>
<h2>Tips for Secret Santa</h2>
<p>Look at what the person already carries. A water bottle in a bag pocket tells you something, and a pair of hair clips on a desk tells you more. Avoid anything that needs a size unless you know it. Keep it plain: a small useful gift with a handwritten note beats a clever one that nobody uses.</p>
<p>If you do not know them well, pick a gift that works for any level: a towel, a bottle, or a gift card to a studio they have mentioned. If you would like to go bigger for someone close, see our <a href="/blogs/news/gifts-for-pilates-lovers">gifts for Pilates lovers</a> and <a href="/blogs/news/gifts-for-barre-and-yoga-people">gifts for barre and yoga people</a>. More from the Journal is on the <a href="/blogs/news">Barreletics Journal</a>.</p>
<h2>Questions people ask about small gifts for studio people</h2>
<h3>What is a good Pilates Secret Santa gift under $50?</h3>
<p>A quick dry towel, a water bottle, hair clips or a small massage ball all fit a $50 limit and need no size. A Barreletics tank at $34 or tee at $39 works if you know the size.</p>
<h3>What are good stocking stuffers for a barre or yoga fan?</h3>
<p>Small items work best: hair ties, a massage ball, bands, a towel. Pick something that fits their routine and does not need a size.</p>
<h3>Can I get Barreletics Performance Skins under $50?</h3>

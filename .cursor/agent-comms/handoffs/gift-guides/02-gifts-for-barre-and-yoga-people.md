```html
<h2 class="article-dek">Gifts for people who live in the studio, whether they hold a plank or a pose.</h2>
<p class="article-lead">Barre and yoga people share a lot of habits: the same studio, the same time slot, the same bag by the door. That makes them easier to shop for than you might think. This guide covers props, small upgrades and a few things to wear, with real prices where the item is ours.</p>
<h2>What makes a good barre or yoga gift</h2>
<p>The best gifts are things that make the weekly routine smoother. A strap that stays put, a bag that holds everything, a pair of shoes that stop the slipping. Skip anything that needs a long explanation.</p>
<p>Barreletics makes some of the picks below, so we flag which ones are ours. The rest are general ideas, with no brand attached, because the right brand depends on what they already own.</p>
<h2>For the barre regular</h2>
<h3>1. Grip shoes made for barre, $74 for most colors</h3>
<p>Barre has a lot of heel lifts, pulses and quick turns. Barreletics Performance Skin is a grip shoe built for that kind of work, and for Pilates and reformer too. Founder Stefanie Miller started the brand after she could not get a grip during barre practice, and she designed the grip into the sole itself. There is no latex and no silicone.</p>
<p>The <a href="/products/best-reformer-pilates-legree-workout-shoes">Closed Sole</a> covers the whole bottom of the foot, heel included. The <a href="/products/studio-performance-skin-footwear">Open Sole</a> leaves the heel open and has a vent at the mid foot for a more barefoot feel. Toes are free in both. Most colors are $74, and a few limited colors are $78. If they are not sure which they would like, the <a href="/pages/compare-open-closed-sole">comparison page</a> sets the two side by side.</p>
<h3>2. Light ankle or wrist weights</h3>
<p>Many barre classes use small weights, and people often buy their own for home. If you go this way, ask them what weight they use in class, or choose a light pair. Heavier is not always better, and a weight that is too heavy tends to end up in a drawer.</p>
<h3>3. A class pack at their studio</h3>
<p>Same advice as always: buy at the studio they already attend. Many barre studios sell packs online, and prices vary a lot between cities. Check before you decide how much to spend.</p>
<h2>For the yoga regular</h2>
<h3>4. A block and a strap</h3>
<p>Blocks and straps are useful at every level, and they are the props people most often borrow from the studio. A set of their own means they can practice at home and stop borrowing the studio's. Cork and foam both work, and cork feels firmer. Choose by what they already use.</p>
<h3>5. A mat towel or a mat cleaner</h3>
<p>A towel that grips the mat helps in sweaty classes, and a spray and cloth keeps a mat clean between classes. Both are small, cheap and welcome. Check the label on a cleaner to make sure it is safe for their mat type before you buy.</p>
<h3>6. A mat carrier</h3>
<p>If they walk or bike to class, a strap or sling makes the trip easier. A pocket for keys and a phone is a nice extra.</p>
<h3>7. A bolster for home</h3>
<p>Restorative and gentle classes often use bolsters, and few people have one at home. It is a bigger gift, so make sure they have space for it.</p>
<p>A word on footwear: if they like practicing barefoot on the mat, they may not want shoes at all. The Barreletics product pages list yoga on or off the mat as a use, so they can wear them for floor work and warm ups if they like the feel. Do not push it on someone who prefers bare feet.</p>
<h2>Clothes they will wear on repeat</h2>
<p>Clothing is a riskier gift than props, because fit is personal. Only buy it if you know their size, and keep the receipt in the box. Here are the Barreletics pieces, with current prices.</p>
<h3>8. A Barreletics tee or tank, $39 or $34</h3>
<p>The <a href="/products/barreletics-performance-fabric-yoga-t-shirts">Performance Fabric T Shirt or Tank Top</a> is a lightweight, loose fit top for movement. The V neck tee is $39 and the tank is $34. Returns are accepted within 30 days if the item is unworn, unwashed and has its tags. Check the <a href="/pages/yoga-pants-t-shirt-size-guide">apparel size guide</a> before you order.</p>
<h3>9. Reinforced knee yoga tights, $89</h3>
<p>The <a href="/products/lightly-padded-knee-yoga-pant-black">Super High Rise Reinforced Knee Yoga Tight</a> is made with reinforced knees for kneeling and floor work. It comes in black and in black and white, and it is $89. The product page suggests sizing down, so use the size guide before you pick a size. If you are unsure, a top is a safer bet than pants.</p>
<h2>If you only buy one thing</h2>
<p>For a barre person, buy grip shoes if you know their size, or a class pack if you do not. For a yoga person, buy a block and strap, or a mat towel. For both, a gift card to their studio is a good fallback. You can see every Barreletics style in the <a href="/collections/all">full shop</a>, and the <a href="/collections/apparel">apparel collection</a> has the tops and tights in one place.</p>
<p>Shopping for a Pilates regular? Read our <a href="/blogs/news/gifts-for-pilates-lovers">gifts for Pilates lovers</a>. Buying for a group or an office swap? See the <a href="/blogs/news/stocking-stuffers-and-secret-santa-gifts-under-50">stocking stuffers and Secret Santa ideas under $50</a>.</p>
<h2>Questions people ask about barre and yoga gifts</h2>
<h3>What is a good gift for someone who goes to barre class?</h3>
<p>A class pack at their studio, light weights, or a pair of grip shoes made for barre. Pick based on what they already own and what they complain about.</p>
<h3>What do you buy a yoga lover who has everything?</h3>
<p>Small, useful items work well: a mat towel, a cleaner, a block or a strap. A class pack at their own studio is also welcome. Avoid a main mat unless they have named the one they want.</p>
<h3>Can you wear Barreletics Performance Skins for yoga?</h3>
<p>The product pages list yoga on or off the mat as a use, along with barre, Pilates and reformer. Some yoga people prefer bare feet on the mat, so ask first.</p>
<h3>How much do the Barreletics apparel pieces cost?</h3>
<p>At the time of writing, the tank top is $34, the V neck tee is $39 and the reinforced knee yoga tights are $89.</p>
<h3>Is clothing a safe gift?</h3>
<p>Only if you know their size. Barreletics apparel can be returned within 30 days if it is unworn, unwashed and has its tags. Use the size guide before you order.</p>
```

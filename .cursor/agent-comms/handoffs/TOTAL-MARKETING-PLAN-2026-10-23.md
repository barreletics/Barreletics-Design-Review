## 2. Diagnosis in plain words

1. **Sales fell about a fifth, and it began before the relaunch.** The relaunch did not break checkout.
2. **Less Meta traffic is the biggest driver.** Meta spend fell 25% but clicks fell 48% (Sep 1 to 24 vs Oct 9 to 22). Click rate fell. That fits tired creative, not higher prices [WHY].
3. **Meta loses money on the first order today.** 0.80x (basis B) on Shopify over 30 days [BASIS]. Break even is 1 divided by margin: 2.0x at 50%, 1.67x at 60%, 1.43x at 70% [ADV]. Margin is not known.

7. **Organic and email barely show up.** Instagram organic 5 orders in 30 days. Email 3. We cannot judge Stefanie's posts without dates and Insights [POSTS].
8. **International demand left with the Meta international test** (paused) [WHY, ACTIVE].

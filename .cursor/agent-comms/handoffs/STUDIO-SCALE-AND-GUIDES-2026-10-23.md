## B4. Mechanisms that replace public codes (best first)

1. **Shopify B2B for studios and shops.** Approved companies see wholesale prices and quantity rules at checkout, no code involved. Works for tiers 3 and 4. Faire Direct is the no work alternative for the buyer who prefers Faire.
2. **Automatic discount for a customer segment** for instructor personal pricing. Shopify supports automatic discounts that apply to a customer segment, for example `customer_tags CONTAINS inst-verified` (VERIFIED earlier from Shopify's Aug 2025 changelog). The customer must be signed in or use an email Shopify recognizes at checkout, so create accounts and tell instructors to sign in. This removes the manual "add email to discount" step. No code string exists to leak.
3. **Per person single use code through Flow plus the Admin API.** This is what `IG-EMAIL-*` and `FB-EMAIL-*` already do. Use it for the audience perk, if Andrew decides there should be one. Each code is bound to the buyer's email, limit 1. Needs a small build (Flow "Send HTTP request" is on Grow and up, checked earlier).
4. **Never:** shared, memorable codes like `SAVE2` for partners. Retire or restrict the six open codes for anything partner related.

**Gated pages.** The apply page can stay public but shows no rates. A private "partner portal" page (personal link, shipping form, images, class kit PDF, terms) is locked by customer tag with an app such as Locksmith (https://apps.shopify.com/locksmith, page loads, tag based locks to be confirmed at install). A password page is the zero cost fallback but one password is shared, so tag locks are better.

**Non guessable personal links.** Each person gets `?ref=` plus a random 8 to 10 character token, for example `barreletics.com/?utm_source=inst&utm_medium=partner&utm_content=k7Qx93Ld`. A token is not discoverable by coupon scrapers, can be revoked per person, and Shopify records it on the order's customer journey (I used the same last touch field in Turn 1). Two ways to run it:
- **Zero cost:** random token links plus a weekly report from `customerJourneySummary` (read only, a bot can do this).
- **UpPromote Professional:** auto discount through the link, anti leak, payouts. $89.99 a month plus 1.5% (needs Andrew's approval). Docs page: https://docs.uppromote.com/management/programs/auto-discounts-with-affiliate-link (loads).

**Important limit on links:** a link is shareable. If a discount rides on the link, it can still be passed around. Keep discounts tied to a signed in person (tag segment) for instructors, and keep audience offers small or non discount (a free gift, early access, free shipping threshold) until abuse data exists.

Whether Flow can read the landing page UTM on an order trigger needs checking in the Flow editor. If not, a nightly bot reads the journey summary and a human or an approved write job applies the tag.

# PART C. Do we need a distributor now?

High level. The Distribution bot owns the detailed strategy (`/workspace/distribution/DISTRIBUTION-STRATEGY-v1.md`). I did not re audit its numbers.

## C1. Short answer

**Probably not as an exclusive distributor, probably yes to testing wholesale channels that do the same job without giving up the studio price.** A brand at roughly $18,000 of real Shopify sales in the last 30 days (Turn 1: 199 orders, $18,028.14) has not shown wholesale demand data in what I read. A distributor needs proof that studios reorder.

## C2. Pros and cons

| | Pros | Cons |
|---|---|---|
| Distributor | Opens accounts you cannot reach, handles freight, invoicing and collections, one order for many studios | Takes a big margin cut ($25 price against $37 studio wholesale per the files), can conflict with your own studio price, wants exclusivity, needs inventory up front, little control of the brand story |
| Direct wholesale (Shopify B2B, Faire Direct) | Keep the $37, direct relationships, easy to start | Your time, small orders, collections, shipping many boxes |
| Sales rep group | Commission only, they know studios and boutiques | Typically 10 to 15% of net wholesale (search sources, UNVERIFIED, no single authoritative page), they need samples and a line sheet, slow to ramp |
| 3PL | Handles pick pack, wholesale and DTC together | Setup and per order fees (no quote checked), minimums |
| Shopify Markets | Sell abroad with local currency and duties without a distributor | You still handle returns and customer service. Needs duty and VAT policy |

## C3. Margin impact (ASSUMPTION level, per pair, gross only)

From B7: studio direct $27.40, distributor $15.40. The distributor gives up $12 per pair compared to direct studio wholesale. In return you save your own selling and shipping costs, and you reach accounts you would not reach. A paid acquired DTC pair has to be compared: the analysis in Turn 1 and the distribution files put paid acquisition costs far above the $15.40 gross, so one reorder from a distributor can beat a paid customer. That comparison needs real CPA by month and was not recomputed here (the $54 to $85 CPA figure is from the Distribution files, not audited).

Faire route: 15% commission plus $10 on the first order from a new customer found on Faire, **0% for retailers you bring with your Faire Direct link** (VERIFIED, Faire help page). So Faire Direct costs nothing on commission for your own studio list and gives the buyer net 60 terms.

## C4. When a distributor makes sense

- Wholesale orders reach a steady pace you cannot ship or invoice yourself.
- Studio reorders prove sell through (reorder rate, units per account).
- The distributor reaches a channel you cannot (international, big box, boutique chains).
- You can hold stock and the distributor commits to volume.
- Territory and price protection are written down.

## C5. International

Start with Shopify Markets or a regional 3PL and a few retail partners. A distributor in one country can make sense once a market sends steady orders (for example if UK or Canada orders grow). The UK gift guide request platforms I found (DIARY) show UK media demand, but the order data does not yet say whether UK buyers exist. Verify country level orders first.

## C6. Numbers someone must verify before deciding

1. Current wholesale orders: count, size, reorder rate, by account.
2. Real landed cost per pair and freight per pair (the $8.95 to $9.60 figure is a planning number).
3. Shipping and 3PL cost per wholesale carton.
4. Rep commission and Faire fees you would actually pay (Faire fees VERIFIED, rep rates not).
5. Studio price floor policy: does a distributor at $25 break the $37 studio promise?
6. Inventory capacity and lead time for a large order.
7. Exclusivity terms and who owns the customer list.
